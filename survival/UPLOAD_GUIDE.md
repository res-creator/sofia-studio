# Last Day Protocol — what to upload and how

## Order
1. **Video 1 — "First 5 Days"** (`ep1/out/zombie_outbreak_ep1.mp4`, 7:19).
2. **Video 2 — "Cruise Ship"**, one week later. Upload ONE version only (two versions of the same story = duplicate content):
   - recommended: the full pilot, 7:00 — `pilot2/out/zombie_outbreak_cruise_full.mp4`
   - alternative: the D2 version, 5:00 — `cruise/out/zombie_outbreak_cruise.mp4`
After that: one new video every week, same day and time.

## When (US audience)
- Best days: **Thursday, Friday, Saturday**. Best time: **12:00–3:00 PM New York time (ET)**.
- ET → Moscow: until Nov 2 (US daylight time) +7 h → 19:00–22:00 MSK; after Nov 2 +8 h → 20:00–23:00 MSK.
- Use "Schedule", not "Publish now": upload the day before, check everything, set the time.
- Suggested: Video 1 — Friday 1:00 PM ET; Video 2 — next Friday 1:00 PM ET.

## Settings for every video (YouTube Studio → Upload)
**Details**
- Title, description, thumbnail as above. Playlist: **"Zombie Outbreak"** (put both videos in it).
- Audience: **No, it's not made for kids** (cartoon horror).
- Show more →
  - Altered or synthetic content: **No** (obviously animated; AI voices of non-real people).
  - Paid promotion: No. Automatic chapters: on (the timestamps already make chapters).
  - Tags: paste from above. Language: **English**. Caption certification: **None — never aired on TV in the US**.
  - Category: **Film & Animation**. License: Standard YouTube License. Comments: on.

**Video elements**
- Subtitles → Add language → English → Upload file → **With timing** → the `.srt` file.
- End screen (last 10–20 s): **Subscribe** button + **"Best for viewer"** video (or the other episode).

**Visibility**
- **Schedule** → date and time from "When" above.

## After publishing
- Pin the comment, answer the first comments in the first hour.
- Post the thumbnail in the Community tab ("New episode — Day 1 vs Day 30").
- Do not edit the title/thumbnail in the first 24 h; after 2–3 days check click-through rate (CTR) in Analytics. If CTR < 3 %, try the alternative title: "I Was Trapped on a Zombie Cruise Ship for 30 Days".
