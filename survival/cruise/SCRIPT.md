# Zombie Outbreak: 30 Days Trapped on a Cruise Ship — final script (v1)

Rules: `../PRODUCTION_RULES.md`. Lines and voices: `script.json` (canonical text). Narrator: Brian (pilot voice).
The hero is "you": a ship's waiter, never speaks. Estimated length **≈ 9:00** (voice 3:15 + action and silence).

## Logline
A waiter barricaded in the galley of a drifting cruise ship realizes that someone keeps restarting the engines and steering away from land. He finds out who — and on day 30 learns that someone else has been on the bridge the whole time.

## Central mystery
**Who is controlling the ship?**
- **Answered in the episode:** Rosa Okafor, the chief engineer. She stopped the ship on the bridge's order on day 1 and keeps it at sea because the Coast Guard sinks infected ships near the coast.
- **New mystery at the end:** someone sealed on the bridge since day 1 (not the captain) takes over the steering on day 30.

## S01 · DAY 1 — The message (0:00–0:40)
**What & why:** an ordinary sunset service breaks down in seconds. **Viewer learns:** emergency; the captain is silenced. **Hero:** carries a tray of champagne, reads a guest's phone, freezes, the tray tilts and glasses shatter as he backs away from the stairway.
- **Camera:** opens on black → one phone screen lights up in extreme close-up (the hook in the first 2 s); hard cut wide: 30 phones light up across the pool deck at once. Locked-off wide during the captain's PA; slow push on the ceiling speaker on "changing course to the nearest—"; click; hold 2 s on the dead speaker. A scream from below: snap zoom to the stairwell.
- **Sound:** silence → one buzz → a swarm of buzzes; ocean, gulls; PA chime; captain cut by a click; total silence; distant scream; the glasses shatter.
- **Ends with:** passengers start running. *Why keep watching:* what happened to the captain, what's below?

## S02 · Deck Five (0:40–1:15)
**What & why:** the hero goes down to take guests back to cabins (his job) and meets the first infected. **Viewer learns:** infected are slow, react to sound, not sight. **Hero:** hides behind a room-service cart; a plate slides off and clatters — heads snap toward it; he flicks a fork far down the corridor — they turn and follow it.
- **Camera:** side tracking along the cabin corridor (search); emergency light flicker; snap zoom on the snapping heads; locked frame while the infected pass inches from the cart (tension); a shadow attack on the wall at the far end (we see the shadow, not the bite).
- **Sound:** carpet footsteps, low groans, ventilation hum; one plate clatter; fork skittering; music off.
- **Ends with:** the corridor is clear but people above are screaming "lifeboats!".

## S03 · Rule number one (1:15–1:45)
**What & why:** everyone runs up; the hero chooses down. **Viewer learns:** the lifeboat deck is a death trap. **Hero:** stops at the deck map ("YOU ARE HERE"), traces with a finger, looks at the crowd, then pushes a "CREW ONLY" door to the service stairs.
- **Camera:** tilt up from the map to the stairwell full of running legs; **snap zoom out** to the lifeboat deck from above: a jammed davit, a crowd, infected inside the crowd (silhouettes, no gore); back to the hero: close on his hand on the CREW ONLY door.
- **Sound:** crowd roar, ship horn, alarms; the crew door closes → muffled silence.
- **Ends with:** darkness of the service stairs.

## S04 · The galley (1:45–2:30)
**What & why:** the safest place he knows. **Viewer learns:** supplies (food 3 weeks, water 9 days — a countdown), one steel door. **Hero:** counts shelves while the narrator lists them, drags a heavy cart against the door, kills the lights; an oven timer goes off — he lunges and smothers it in a towel; bangs on the door stop... a slow scrape... silence. Later he sits on the floor, back to the cart.
- **Camera:** pan across the shelves (inventory, each item touched by the hero); locked-off wide during the bangs (no music); tilt down to the hanging ladles — they swing with the ship... and stop: the engines have stopped.
- **Sound:** fridge hum, timer ring (shocking in silence), bangs, scrape; the engine vibration bed fades out to nothing.
- **Ends with:** the engines stop. Nobody explains why.

## S05 · DAYS 2–5 — The rhythm (2:30–3:10)
**What & why:** survival becomes routine; the routine is broken by a clue. **Viewer learns:** the ship drifts; the hero rations; someone else is moving around. **Hero:** draws tally marks on the fridge with a marker, measures water with tape marks on the bottle, listens at the ceiling; on day 5 opens the cold room — the bread tray is half empty, the door had been closed, there is a black oil handprint on the inside handle.
- **Camera:** locked frames that change only by time (tally marks multiply, light through the porthole moves = time passing); slow tilt up to the ceiling with the footsteps; snap zoom on the oil handprint.
- **Sound:** footsteps above at noon, dragging at midnight, long silences; the cold-room door seal.
- **Ends with:** "the infected don't close doors behind them". *Someone living has a way in.*

## S06 · DAY 7 — The night visitor (3:10–3:55)
**What & why:** he decides to watch instead of confront. **Viewer learns:** a living crew member in coveralls uses a service hatch in the cold room; she's careful and armed with a flashlight. **Hero:** hides behind the steel counter with a frying pan; holds his breath when the flashlight beam sweeps over the counter; after she leaves, finds a dropped wristband: ENGINEERING · R. OKAFOR. The hatch is labelled "SVC TUNNEL → ENG".
- **Camera:** one long locked-off night frame (6–8 s of nothing but the fridge hum) — the panel in the cold room slides; flashlight beam cuts the dark; the hero's eyes in close-up as the beam passes; tilt down to the wristband on the floor.
- **Sound:** silence, fridge hum, metal panel scrape, careful steps, the hero's held breath; the hatch closing.
- **Ends with:** he doesn't follow — too dangerous — but now he knows the way.

## S07 · DAY 9 — The last bottle (3:55–4:50)
**What & why:** the countdown from s04 hits zero; he must go out. **Viewer learns:** the corridor layout; the infected stand still in the dark until a sound wakes them; the Coast Guard will use force. **Hero:** opens the barricade quietly, walks forty steps (POV), passes infected standing motionless; in the crew mess fills bottles from the ice machine — the machine roars; infected turn; he grabs a handheld radio from a table and runs back; slams the cart into place.
- **Camera:** hero POV down the corridor (slow, the only POV of the episode besides the tunnel); locked frame on a motionless infected's back while the hero passes; **fast cuts** for the escape; locked wide back in the galley; push on the radio as it crackles.
- **Sound:** breathing, carpet steps, a dripping pipe; the ice machine grind (loud, sudden); running; door slam; radio static → Coast Guard message.
- **Ends with:** the message: any infected ship approaching the coast will be stopped by force.

## S08 · Night 10 — The speakers (4:50–5:10)
**What & why:** an eerie beat that makes the ship feel watched. **Viewer learns:** someone has access to the PA system. **Hero:** freezes on his mat, looks up at the ceiling speaker, slowly reaches for the frying pan.
- **Camera:** locked frame on the speaker; slow push; hold until the click.
- **Sound:** PA click on → quiet breathing through the speakers for 3 s → click off. No music.
- **Ends with:** silence. Someone is listening.

## S09 · DAY 12 — The light (5:10–5:45)
**What & why:** the drift brings them near land — hope. **Viewer learns:** someone deliberately turns the ship away. **Hero:** sees a lighthouse through the porthole, grabs a flashlight to signal — and stops (the Coast Guard words). Then the floor vibrates: engines. The lighthouse slides out of the porthole as the ship turns.
- **Camera:** close on the porthole with the blinking light; the hero's hand on the flashlight switch (hesitation, locked frame); the porthole view rotates (the ship turning) — the light slides out of frame; **snap zoom out** to the whole ship at night turning away from the coast.
- **Sound:** waves, the lighthouse beam (no sound) — then the deep engine start-up rumble; the ladles start swinging again (callback to s04).
- **Ends with:** "Someone else is alive on this ship. And they don't want to be rescued."

## S10 · The service tunnel (5:45–6:30)
**What & why:** the hero chooses to find the driver. **Viewer learns:** the tunnels connect the ship; infected can be inside too. **Hero:** packs (flashlight, frying pan, the kitchen timer, the wristband), opens the hatch with the wristband's key tag, crawls into the tunnel; hears crawling behind him; winds the kitchen timer and slides it into a side duct — the ringing moves away, the infected crawler follows it; he continues in silence and drops into the engine room.
- **Camera:** close-ups of packing (each object = earlier setup); tunnel POV with the flashlight cone; locked frame looking back down the tunnel at a shape crawling toward him; fast cuts as he winds and slides the timer; still frame while the ring fades away.
- **Sound:** metal knocking under knees, his breathing; a second set of scraping behind him; the timer's ring travelling away; the engine room roar when he drops in.
- **Ends with:** a flare gun pointed at his face.

## S11 · Engine control (6:30–7:35)
**What & why:** the standoff becomes an alliance; the main question is answered. **Viewer learns:** who drives the ship and why; the bridge ordered "all stop" and has been sealed since day 1. **Hero:** raises his hands, shows the wristband, then — slowly — a loaf of bread from his bag as a peace offering. Rosa lowers the flare gun.
- **Camera:** locked two-shot standoff in red emergency light; close-ups only on the flare gun, the wristband, the bread; slow push on Rosa as she explains; a tilt up to the console "STEERING: ENGINE CONTROL" when she says she takes the ship back out.
- **Sound:** engine room drone (low, constant), the console beeps; no music until the alliance, then a quiet warm theme.
- **Ends with:** "Not a ghost. A very tired engineer." — the mystery solved, and a seed planted: *nobody has opened the bridge since day one.*

## S12 · DAYS 13–29 — Two survivors (7:35–8:10)
**What & why:** a short breath before the end; new stake: fuel. **Viewer learns:** they cooperate; fuel is running out; tomorrow they must pick a direction. **Hero:** cooks on a camping stove in the engine room, hands Rosa a plate; she shows him the fuel gauge; tally marks on a pipe go up to 29.
- **Camera:** three locked compositions repeated with small changes (same frame, different day) — cheap and effective; tilt down to the fuel gauge needle dropping.
- **Sound:** quiet warm theme, stove hiss, engine drone; the theme stops on "fuel for one more day".
- **Ends with:** Rosa: "Tomorrow we pick a direction. And we don't come back."

## S13 · DAY 30 — The bridge (8:10–9:10)
**What & why:** the payoff of the captain's cut-off line and the PA clicks. **Viewer learns:** someone has been alive on the bridge all along, not the captain, and he has now taken the helm. **Hero:** stares at the console as the rudder indicator moves by itself; looks at the bridge camera monitor; when the PA clicks on he grips the frying pan; at the end he and Rosa look at each other — then he looks up at the ceiling speaker.
- **Camera:** close on the console: "STEERING: ENGINE CONTROL" flips to "BRIDGE — OVERRIDE"; snap zoom on the rudder indicator turning; the CCTV monitor flickers: a dark bridge, a figure in a captain's coat at the wheel, back to camera, a bandaged hand — **slow push** on the monitor; the figure begins to turn its head — the monitor cuts to static before we see the face. PA voice over a locked wide of the two survivors. Final shot: snap zoom out from the engine room through the ship to the whole liner at dawn turning toward an unknown horizon; title card.
- **Sound:** silence before the switch; one beep; engines rising; the PA click from s08 (same sound, so the viewer recognises it); the calm voice; a long silence; then the engines at full power and a single low musical note under the title.
- **Ends with (final narration):** "You found out who was driving. Now you just have to find out where he's taking you." Title card with the episode name. **No "TO BE CONTINUED".**

## Cause-and-effect check (done)
- Captain wanted a port ("nearest—"), yet the bridge ordered **all stop** → the first contradiction; explained only in s13 (someone else took the bridge).
- Water for nine days (s04) → day 9 forced trip (s07) → the radio is found on that trip → the Coast Guard warning → the hero does not signal the lighthouse (s09) and Rosa's motive makes sense (s11).
- Bread, oil handprint, wristband (s05–s06) → the hero knows a living engineer exists and has the key to the tunnel (s10) → Rosa trusts him because he returns her wristband and brings bread (s11).
- The timer nearly gave him away (s04) → he uses the same weakness as a weapon (s10).
- The PA clicks (s08) → the same click introduces the bridge voice (s13): "thank you for keeping us alive" = he was listening the whole time.
- Fuel lasted 30 days because the ship mostly drifted; Rosa ran the engines only near the coast (s11) → fuel for one day on day 29 (s12) → the bridge takes over exactly when the last fuel decides the destination (s13).

## Technical plausibility (fixed in v1.1)
- **Power vs. propulsion:** a cruise ship's diesel generators make electricity; the propulsion motors are separate. "All stop" stops the propellers, the lights, fridges and the crew-mess ice machine keep working (s04: "The lights stay on"). Potable water comes from the ship's tanks, which is why the ice machine still makes ice on day 9.
- **Coast Guard information:** the hero takes a handheld **marine VHF radio** (from a lifeboat kit on the crew-mess wall); the Coast Guard broadcasts on **channel 16**, the international distress channel. Rosa hears the same broadcasts on the engine-control VHF.
- **Why Rosa keeps away from land:** the broadcasts say infected vessels will be stopped by force, and she has no way to prove the ship is clean. She can't reach the bridge, so she steers with the **emergency (local) steering** in the steering-gear room and runs the engines only when the drift brings them close to the coast — that is also why the fuel lasts 30 days.
- **Day 30:** the bridge can override local steering because the bridge console always has priority once someone takes command there.

## Removed in the review (weak or repetitive)
- A second chase on the lifeboat deck (replaced by one wide reveal shot in s03).
- A flashback showing how the captain was attacked (it would answer the new mystery too early).
- A scene where the hero signals the lighthouse and is shot at (too big for the budget, and the radio already establishes the threat).
- Comic overlays and a "duck"-style running gag (rule: serious tone, dry narrator humor only).
