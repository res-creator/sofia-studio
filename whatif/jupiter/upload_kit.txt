Facts used (checked):
- Jupiter has no solid surface; mostly hydrogen (~90% of molecules) and helium (~10%).
- Falling from far away you hit the atmosphere at ~60 km/s (Jupiter's escape velocity ≈ 59.5 km/s). NY–LA ≈ 3,940 km ≈ 66 s at that speed.
- Cloud tops ≈ -145 °C; upper clouds are ammonia ice.
- Great Red Spot: a storm wider than Earth.
- Galileo probe (Dec 1995) sent data for ~58 minutes before heat and pressure ended it.
- Deep inside, hydrogen becomes liquid metallic hydrogen; near the core it may reach ~20,000 °C, far above the Sun's surface (~5,500 °C).
