# KNOWN IN ADVANCE — "The Morning Mount St. Helens Exploded Sideways": full-film block plan

- **Target length:** ≈ 14 min, including the approved opening of 1:30.
- **Facts:** `research/facts.md`. Source codes: PP1249 = USGS "The First 100 Days of Mount St. Helens"; PP1250 = USGS Prof. Paper 1250; B1383C = Crandell & Mullineaux 1978; FS036 = USGS fact sheet.
- **Materials:** `research/materials.md` and `research/materials_people.md`.
- **Channel line:** the channel name means "the signs were there". It does NOT mean "they knew exactly what would happen". Every block keeps two things apart: what was observed then, and what became clear only afterward.

## Coverage check before the voice-over
- **Every block has its own archival material.** Repeats are deliberate and carry a new meaning:
  - The May 17 photo returns as the before/after pair from the same viewpoint.
  - The relief map returns in blocks 3, 5, 6 and 8, each time with a new layer: flank → positions → avalanche path → blast fan.
  - The Johnston photo appears only in the opening and once in block 8, inside the USGS film's own cut under Swanson's words.
- **Documents:** four documents in different blocks: B1383C, PP1249 p. 35, PP1249 p. 41, PP1249 p. 7. Plus the PP1250 quote as text, to avoid over-using pages.
- **Gaps (no reusable material):**
  - The collapse itself: covered by the labelled reconstruction.
  - Harry Truman: copyrighted photos, so text card only.
  - TV news of 1980.
  - The real rescue film, depending on the people search.
