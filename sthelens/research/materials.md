## Found, not used in the opening (for the full film)
- **D** `1980_04_17_USGS_MSH_FW.mp4`: 6 min static view of the north side (Dogs Head, the bulge, Goat Rocks) with emissions.
- **D** `1980_05_13_Est_USGS_MSH_TL_Clip_4.mp4`: zoomed time-lapse on the bulge. It is washed out and purple, so it was replaced in the cut.
- **V** `view-north-mount-st-helens-eruption-and-first-crater.jpg`: the first crater (David Frank).
- **L** Rest of the data release (CSV lists ~80 pre-May-18 clips and the May 18–Dec 1980 section). Key ones:
  - `1980_05_18_USGS_MSH_FW` (17 min, "probably from the May 18 eruption", 1.9 GB);
  - `1980_05_24–26` and `05_28`–`05_31`: blast zone, blowdown, Spirit Lake full of logs, scientists in the field — for the "consequences" section.
- **L** USGS photo pages for May 18 (eruption column, lahars, Muddy River bridge, ash).

## Needed but not legally available (copyrighted)
- **The collapse itself at 8:32:**
  - Photo sequences by Gary Rosenquist and by Keith Ronnholm (private copyright). No public-domain film of the first seconds is known to us.
  - In the full film the mechanism must be explained with a **labelled schematic** on the USGS DEM, plus the USGS diagrams in the CoreCast film. Those diagrams were made by USGS graphics staff, so their reuse is probably OK.
- **TV news of 1980** (KGW, KOIN, network news): copyrighted. It would need a licence or a fair-use decision by the owner.
- **Audio of Johnston's call:** no recording is known to us. The quote is shown as text with a source and is not imitated.

## Notes and checks
- Banks says "about six feet a day". The USGS caption says "up to five feet (1.5 m) per day". The narration avoids the number and lets Banks speak; the full film should mention the USGS figure.
- Coldwater II position: approximate (Johnston Ridge, ~6 mi from the summit). It is marked "positions approximate" on the map.
