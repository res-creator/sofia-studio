# Materials: people and rescue (Mount St. Helens, 1980)

Searched on 2026-11-03. Covers rescue, people in the zone, aftermath with people, archival audio and NARA film.

**Status:** L = link only, D = downloaded, V = downloaded and viewed (I looked at the image, or for video at a contact sheet of frames).

**Local root:** `sthelens/assets/raw/people/` (gitignored). Total downloaded: about 1.0 GB.

**Rights basics:**
- Photos and film made by US federal employees (Air Force, Army, USFS, FEMA, USGS) are public domain under 17 USC 105.
- The NARA catalog's `useRestriction` field is quoted for each item.
- "Newspaper photo", "copyrighted" or a third-party credit overrides any "Public Domain" label.

**Sources searched:**
- **NARA catalog:** the JSON proxy `catalog.archives.gov/proxy/records/search` worked. The `/api/v2` endpoint needs a key.
- **USGS:** media pages, found through the usgs.gov sitemap.
- **ScienceBase:** USGS historical film, CC0.
- **Web search:** for af.mil, WA State Archives and DVIDS.

**Sources blocked or skipped:**
- **Wikimedia Commons:** returned HTTP 429 three times, so I skipped it.
- **DVIDS:** returned a 202 bot challenge with an empty body. Skipped; I did not bypass it.
- **af.mil photo pages:** returned 403. They hold the same Leach 304th photos as NARA anyway.

**Rescue facts found:**
- The 304th ARRS (Portland IAP) flew 111 sorties and saved 61 people, out of 101 saves recorded by the AFRCC.
- Sources: the 920th Rescue Wing "Flashback Friday" page (https://www.920rqw.afrc.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/678416/flashback-friday-a-blast-from-the-past) and https://www.rotorheadsrus.us/documents/1980_2015.html.
- I could not open either page myself (af.mil returned 403), so treat these figures as unverified.

**Not found in reusable form:**
- Any photo or film of an actual survivor pickup: a hoist, survivors being loaded, or the Toutle/Green River survivors.
- Any 304th ARRS motion film.
- Any National Guard photos.
- Possible sources of these: NARA 330 or 342-USAF motion picture accessions (not digitized), and the Christopher Anderson/Spokesman-Review photo of the National Guard crew searching, May 19 (copyrighted).

## 4. Archival audio

- **No digitized public-domain audio from May 18, 1980 was found** (NARA, USGS).
- NARA does hold a USFS series, "Sound Recordings Relating to Mount Saint Helens", NAID 581220 (95-MSHa). Its description: "audio recordings that document the eruption… as recorded by forest service personnel and news media personnel… press conferences".

  - **"304th Air Rescue"** 95-MSHa-6, NAID 581322 (reel; catalog date 1980-01-15, which is probably an error).

  - "Aircheck of KGW Radio…" 95-MSHa-14, NAID 581339 (news media, copyrighted).

  - "Volcano Press Conference" 95-MSHa-10, NAID 581331.
