Pick ONE format for the campaign (a video longer than 8 s, OR a photo post with at least 3 photos). Post the other one later as a normal post.

## How to submit
1. TikTok → Inbox → System notifications → Monetization → Campaigns → «Show off your "attitude"!» → **Submit**.
2. Upload the video (or the 4 photos as a photo post).
3. Add the sound **«Bad News»** from the campaign card.
   - For the video: turn the original sound down to about 30 % so the song leads. Our chair-slide whoosh, the newspaper SLAM and the pats stay faintly audible.
4. Paste the caption below and publish.
5. **Keep the post public for at least 10 days**, otherwise it won't count.
