# «MAKE или DO» — два текста по выбору владелицы (начала №2 «Торт» и №6 «Зачем два слова?»)

Стиль для Suno (оба): Bouncy pop-rap, 96 BPM, punchy drums, hard kick and snare, warm Rhodes keys, plucked bass, finger snaps and claps, playful brass stabs, deadpan low male voice in Russian and bright playful female voice in English, call and response, every word on the beat, catchy sing-along chorus, no long intro
Exclude: ballad, slow, dark, metal, long intro

## Версия А — «Торт»
[Intro – spoken over the beat]
[Male] Я сегодня did a cake!
[Female] You DID a cake?!
[Male] Ну... испёк.
[Female] You MADE a cake! Okay. Sit down.

[Verse 1 – call and response]
[Male] Испечь торт?
[Female] Make a cake!
[Male] Заварить чай?
[Female] Make some tea!
[Male] Сварить кофе?
[Female] Make a coffee!
[Male] Приготовить завтрак?
[Female] Make breakfast for me!

[Pre-Chorus]
[Male] А посуду после — тоже «мейк»?
[Female] No, no, no! Do the dishes!
Do is work you have to do,
Make is something brand new!

[Chorus]
[Female] If you cook it — make, make, make!
Make a cake and make some tea!
If you clean it — do, do, do!
Do the dishes after me!
[Male and Female] Make is new and do is work,
Sing it once and it will work!

[Verse 2 – call and response]
[Male] Помыть посуду?
[Female] Do the dishes!
[Male] Постирать?
[Female] Do the laundry!
[Male] Убраться?
[Female] Do the cleaning!
[Male] Сходить за покупками?
[Female] Do the shopping!
[Male] И всё это я?
[Female] Do your best, honey!

[Bridge – spoken, the twist]
[Male] Стоп! Заправить кровать — это уборка. Значит, do the bed?
[Female] Nope. Make the bed!
[Male] Почему?!
[Female] Well... English has a sense of humour.
[Male] Понял. Исключение.

[Chorus – all together]
[Male and Female] If you cook it — make, make, make!
Make a cake and make some tea!
If you clean it — do, do, do!
Do the dishes after me!
Make is new and do is work,
Sing it once and it will work!

[Outro – spoken]
[Male] Торт будешь?
[Female] Did you make it?
[Male] ...Мама сделала.
[Female] Ha! Your mum made it!
[Male] А я сделал... вид.
[Female] You made it up!

[End]

## Версия Б — «Зачем два слова?»
[Intro – spoken over the beat]
[Male] По-русски одно слово — «делать». А по-английски два. Зачем?!
[Female] Because English loves drama!
[Male] И как их различать?
[Female] Watch this!

[Verse 1 – call and response]
[Male] Делать уроки?
[Female] Do your homework!
[Male] Делать работу?
[Female] Do your job!
[Male] Делать зарядку?
[Female] Do exercise!
[Male] Делать всё возможное?
[Female] Do your best, that's right!

[Pre-Chorus]
[Male] Значит, «делать» — это do?
[Female] Wait for it... not always true!

[Verse 2 – call and response]
[Male] Сделать ошибку?
[Female] Make a mistake!
[Male] Сделать выбор?
[Female] Make a choice!
[Male] Наделать шуму?
[Female] Make some noise!
[Male] Заработать денег?
[Female] Make money!
[Male] О, вот это мне нравится!
[Female] Now you're talking!

[Chorus]
[Female] Do — it's a job, it's a task,
Make — something new, don't ask!
Do your homework, do your best,
Make a choice and make a mess!
[Male and Female] Do or make — no more stress,
English loves a little mess!

[Bridge – spoken, the drama]
[Male] Ага! А «принять решение» — это do a decision?
[Female] Drama! Make a decision!
[Male] А «сделать комплимент»?
[Female] Pay a compliment.
[Male] ЧТО?! Третье слово?!
[Female] I told you. English loves drama!

[Chorus – all together]
[Male and Female] Do — it's a job, it's a task,
Make — something new, don't ask!
Do your homework, do your best,
Make a choice and make a mess!
Do or make — no more stress,
English loves a little mess!

[Outro – spoken]
[Male] Ладно. Драма... но я запомнил.
[Female] Drama queen!
[Male] Сама такая.

[End]
