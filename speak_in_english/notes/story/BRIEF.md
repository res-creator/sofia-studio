# Brief: STORY formats for the TikTok channel @speak_in_english (cartoon, Russian-speaking viewers in KZ, BY, UA, US)
Owner's insight: the "lesson" formats are tired; people watch for a STORY with a plot they follow; English must be woven in naturally (not the point of the video). Our past hits were ~2 min long with avg watch 35–43 s. Research verdicts (read for rules): /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-sofia-studio/71199301-3950-5174-9af4-397e27927b12/review2/HOOKS.md (first 3 s rules: cut to a close-up with the key object at 0.4 s, first line ≤6 words and a question/punch, say "по-английски/на английском" early only if natural) and review2/SCRIPTS.md (comment bait: "two camps" or "tell your own story", not "write 1–4").
The three formats are SEPARATE (do not mix them):
A) «Выбери, что будет дальше» — a story stops at a fork, viewers vote in comments; the next episode follows the majority. English: the heroine's options at the fork are English lines (with Russian subtitles), and the consequences show what each line means to a native.
B) «Ты…» (second person) — "Ты проснулся в аэропорту…": realistic, could happen to anyone: delayed/cancelled flight, not allowed to board, lost passport, lost luggage, missed connection, wrong gate, police/customs question; YOU have weak English and must communicate. Tension hour by hour, a twist at the end. English: each scene has the one phrase that saves you (and what happens with broken English). Narrator speaks Russian in 2nd person; the world/people speak English.
C) «Истории подписчиков» — a real viewer's story turned into a cartoon, ending «Пришли свою историю — следующая серия про тебя». We do NOT have real stories yet and must never present an invented story as a real viewer's. So: write the FORMAT TEMPLATE (structure, timings, how the viewer's comment is shown, how English enters: the phrase they didn't know + how a native would say it, consent line), and ONE pilot clearly framed as the owner's own / the channel host's story («История от автора канала»), which ends with the call to send stories.
General rules: 1:30–2:10 long; both UK and US settings are fine (label country); English only inside the cartoon world (signs, people), Russian narrator; no emoji/♥ in video; no single raised finger; no hands behind head; no brand names; hashtags lowercase; no English mistakes presented as correct (broken English is fine when shown as the heroine's struggle and then corrected); a cliffhanger/twist; quotable last line.
Script format (Russian with English lines in quotes, stage directions in *(italics)*):
**<N>. <Title>** (формат A/B/C, страна)
- Обложка: «line1 / **LINE2 (pink, ≤3 words)** / line3»
- 0–3 с: first frame (close-up object) + first line
- Beats with timecodes: what we see *(gag/tension)*, who says what (English + Russian subtitle), narrator line
- Развилка / поворот / финал
- Вопрос для комментариев (two camps / your story) + end screen text
- Длина ~NN s ; Озвучка ~NNN знаков (all spoken text)
