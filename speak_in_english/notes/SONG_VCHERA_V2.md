# «Вчера» v2 — больше неправильных глаголов (~45), припевы-чанты по группам звучания, чтобы хотелось петь
Вайб: Feduk / Элджей. Группы для чантов: -ank/-ung (drink, sing, ring, swim, begin...), -ew/-own (know, throw, fly, grow, blow), -ought (think, buy, bring, fight, catch, teach).

## Style of music (Suno)
Рэп, Trap, 808 басс, мужской вокал, тусовка, хит для тик-тока
## Exclude styles
джаз, саксофон, гитара, лоу-фай

## Lyrics
[Intro – spoken, male, hungover]
Вчера была вечеринка. Сегодня — неправильные глаголы.

[Verse 1 – male rap]
Yesterday I went out — надел лучший прикид,
I took a cab, I paid — и кошелёк забыт.
I met my friends, we sat, we ate, we spoke,
We drank, we sang, I told my worst joke.
I saw her pic, I felt, I thought, I knew —
Я взял телефон, и пальцы сами: «I miss you».
I wrote, I sent, I sent it once again —
Тринадцать раз. And then I fell asleep. Amen.

[Hook – chant, catchy, loud, group vocals]
Drink — drank — drunk, sing — sang — sung,
Ring — rang — rung, swim — swam — swum,
Begin — began — begun, come — came — come —
Я звонил бывшей в два. What's done is done!

[Verse 2 – male rap]
I woke up at noon, I found my phone,
I read her text — and I froze like stone.
«Who is this?» — I hid, I shook, I ran,
Под одеялом прятался весь день, как партизан.
Then mom came in, she brought me tea,
She sat, she said: «What did you do?» — and looked at me.
I got up, I stood, I told the truth:
«Учил английский, мам!» — and that's the proof.

[Hook – chant, catchy, loud, group vocals]
Drink — drank — drunk, sing — sang — sung,
Ring — rang — rung, swim — swam — swum,
Begin — began — begun, come — came — come —
Я звонил бывшей в два. What's done is done!

[Bridge – chant, everyone sings]
Go — went — gone, know — knew — known,
Throw — threw — thrown, fly — flew — flown,
Grow — grew — grown, blow — blew — blown —
Она не отвечает. И я опять alone.
Think — thought, buy — bought, bring — brought,
Fight — fought, catch — caught, teach — taught —
Мама: «Ну хоть чему-то вечеринка научила».
Lesson learned — and lesson taught!

[Final Hook – chant, catchy, loud, group vocals]
Drink — drank — drunk, sing — sang — sung,
Ring — rang — rung, swim — swam — swum,
Begin — began — begun, come — came — come —
Я звонил бывшей в два. What's done is done!

[Outro – spoken, male]
Go — went — gone. Как и моя гордость.

[End]
