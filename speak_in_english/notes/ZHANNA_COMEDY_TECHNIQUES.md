# Zhanna series — comedy techniques borrowed from the Sci-Fi Sitcom Toon guide

Nov 24, 2026. The owner tried the Sci-Fi Sitcom Toon look (PR #154, 19.5 s trial) and rejected it: **our own toon v4 style stays.**
She asked us to take only what is useful from that guide.

What we take is the comedy grammar. The look stays ours: no boiling outlines, no drawing on twos, no portal.

Source: Lemo-Opuscar `styles/scifi-toon/STYLE.md`. We follow its ideas; its text is not copied.

## Apply in every new Zhanna episode

1. **Cold silence before the punchline.** Hold 1.2–2 s of silence before the key line (Petrovich's «Угу», a price reveal).
   - No music, no captions, no chips on screen.
   - Only quiet room tone: fridge hum, clock tick.
   - Nothing moves except breathing and blinks.
   - A very slow push-in on the two-shot.
   - Clear the captions before the silent beat so the pause plays on a clean screen.
2. **Reaction close-up after every absurd line.** Zhanna says something outrageous → hard cut to the victim's face: Kaylee, the cashier, the bride.
   - The laugh lives on the reaction face.
   - Hold it for at least 1.2 s.
3. **Hard cuts only between shots.** No dissolves. A cut to a close-up on an interruption reads funnier than a camera move.
4. **Shake on one word only.** A short shake on the single key word (a scream, "That's not a skill!"), never on a whole line.
5. **Acting over moving.** In held poses add small life:
   - pupils sliding to the side;
   - a gulp;
   - a sweat drop;
   - one eyebrow going up;
   - fingers drumming on the clutch.

   Prefer these over big gestures, and use one per held shot.
6. **Anticipation before every gesture.** A small move the opposite way (~0.15 s) before the arm goes up or the finger points.
7. **Deadpan vs panic duo.** Zhanna is calm with heavy lids; the other character panics. The joke is the gap between the two reactions, so do not let Zhanna panic too.
8. **Rule of three → fast.** Two slow wrong answers from Zhanna, then a third one that is quick and the worst, cut on the beat.
9. **Callback ending.** The last line echoes the first line or the hook, so the video loops. This also helps TikTok watch-time.

## Caption timing rule

- Hold each caption for max(speech + 0.35 s, 1.2 s).
- Never under 1.2 s; that is the owner's rule.
- Clip captions at a cut only if the next shot is a silent beat.

## Not taken

- Line boil.
- Characters drawn on twos (12 fps). The owner wants smooth motion.
- Portals and glow.
- The other character designs.
- Thick purple-black ink.
- The retro terminal tags.
