Тема: he / she / it + S. Мелодичная тёплая читка, распевный припев, без лирической тяжести.

## Title
Добавь S

Melodic Russian rap, laid-back reggae groove, warm dub bass, soft drums, offbeat keys, raspy warm male voice, melodic flow, sung hook, summer vibe, 90 BPM, clear diction, vocals upfront

## Exclude
club, EDM, chant, gang vocals, heavy autotune, trumpet, brass, jazz, saxophone, guitar, lo-fi, boom bap, female vocal

## Чему учит
- he / she / it + S: wakes, makes, works, takes, reads, answers, dances, watches, loves, knows, goes, likes
- es после -ch / -s / -o: watches, dances, goes
- вопрос и отрицание: Does she…? / She doesn't… (S уходит в does)
- I / you / we / they — без S: I love her
