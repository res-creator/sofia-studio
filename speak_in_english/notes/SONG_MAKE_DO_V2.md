# «MAKE или DO» — текст v2 (с историей, исключением и сильным припевом) + запрос для Suno

## Style of music
Bouncy pop-rap, 96 BPM, punchy drums, hard kick and snare, warm Rhodes keys, plucked bass, finger snaps and claps, playful brass stabs, deadpan low male voice in Russian and bright playful female voice in English, call and response, every word on the beat, catchy sing-along chorus, no long intro

## Exclude styles
ballad, slow, dark, metal, long intro

## Lyrics
[Intro – spoken over the beat]
[Male] Ну всё. Английский меня сломал.
[Female] Relax. It's simple.
[Male] Да ладно?
[Female] Go on. Try me.

[Verse 1 – call and response]
[Male] Делать уроки?
[Female] Do your homework!
[Male] Мыть посуду?
[Female] Do the dishes!
[Male] Стирка, спорт?
[Female] Do the laundry, do some sport!
[Male] Сделай милость?
[Female] Do me a favour, of course!

[Pre-Chorus]
[Male] Всё на «ду»! А «мейк» зачем?
[Female] Listen up, it's easy, friend:
Do is work you have to do,
Make is something brand new!

[Chorus]
[Female] Make a cake, make some tea,
Make a wish and make it with me!
Do the dishes, do your best,
Do your homework, then you rest!
[Male and Female] Make if something's new,
Do if it's work for you!

[Verse 2 – call and response]
[Male] Ошибиться?

[Male] Позвонить?

[Male] Решиться?

[Male] Заработать?

[Male] Стоп! Заправить кровать — это же работа. Значит, do the bed?

[Male] Но там же ничего нового!
[Female] Well... English has a sense of humour.

[Chorus – all together]
[Male and Female] Make a cake, make some tea,
Make a wish and make it with me!
Do the dishes, do your best,
Do your homework, then you rest!
Make if something's new,
Do if it's work for you!

[Outro – spoken]

[Female] Simple-ish.

[Female] Почти.

[End]

## Что выучит зритель (16 выражений)
DO: homework, the dishes, the laundry, sport, a favour, your best, nothing.
MAKE: a cake, tea, a wish, a mistake, friends, a call, a decision, money, the bed (исключение!).
Идиомы: Now you're talking! (вот это другой разговор), Simple-ish (почти просто).
