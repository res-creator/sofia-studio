DESCRIPTION
On August 8, 2023, a fire that had been declared contained came back and destroyed the historic town of Lahaina, Maui, in a single afternoon. 102 people died. More than 2,000 buildings were damaged or destroyed.

Maui has 80 outdoor warning sirens. Not one of them sounded.

This documentary follows the whole day, minute by minute: the dry grass, the warnings nobody put together, the morning fire, the rekindle, the gridlock, the people who ran into the ocean, the silent sirens, the hydrants that ran dry, and what the investigations found.
