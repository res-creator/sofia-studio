None of this was a secret. In 2014, a wildfire protection plan for West Maui was signed. News reports say it rated Lahaina's fire risk as extreme. In August 2018, as Hurricane Lane approached, a fire broke out in the Kawaula Valley just south of town. It burned 1,500 acres and damaged or destroyed 21 homes. A deputy fire chief called the conditions unprecedented in the department's history. Years later, investigators found something striking. The fire department and the police never even started a formal review of the 2018 fire. The emergency agency never finished one. Then came August 2023. Far to the south, Hurricane Dora was passing the islands at a safe distance. To the north sat a strong high-pressure system. Between them, the air was squeezed into a powerful river of wind. On August 7th, the National Weather Service issued a red flag warning. Its wind warnings said the gusts could blow down trees and power lines. The next day, gusts reached 80 miles an hour.
