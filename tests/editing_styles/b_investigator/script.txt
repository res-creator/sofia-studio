In 1822, a man sold bonds in London... for a country that did not exist.
Let's look at the evidence.
Exhibit A: a two hundred thousand pound loan, offered to London investors.
Exhibit B: land. One hundred acres, for just over eleven pounds.
Exhibit C: the Bank of Poyais note. Printed in Scotland. Backed by nothing.
Settlers traded their gold for these. About two hundred and fifty sailed.
They found jungle. Fewer than fifty came home.
The man behind it? Gregor MacGregor. A French court acquitted him.
He died in Venezuela, with a military pension and a hero's funeral.
The country was fake. The money was very real.
