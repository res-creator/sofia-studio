# Editing-style test A — "map documentary" (techniques only, no branding of any channel)
16:9 1920x1080, 30 fps, 40 s. Story: Poyais (facts from poyais_ep29/facts.md). Free local voice: Kokoro am_michael (no ElevenLabs credits).

Techniques shown
- 3D-ish globe (orthographic projection of world-atlas 50m) zooming from the Atlantic into the Mosquito Coast, rim shading, graticule
- Paper texture multiplied over the whole map, film grain (overlay), vignette
- Taped sticky notes (1822, POYAIS, ~250 SETTLERS, NO CAPITAL / NO FARMS / JUST JUNGLE, FEWER THAN 50), pop with overshoot
- Hand-written annotations that write on (Caveat), hand-drawn arrows with heads, red marker circles with overshoot ("?" at the coast, "Cazique of Poyais", "St Joseph")
- Country highlight (Honduras) + "Honduras (today)" label
- Archive cut-outs with white borders, drop shadows and parallax on a cardboard desk (portrait, banknote, 1822 engraving)
- Push-in onto the banknote detail, pull-back to the "that's him!" arrow
- Highlighter sweeping line by line over the guidebook quote (re-created page)
- Map close-up: pin drop with label ST. JOSEPH -> crossed out with marker -> jungle grows over the coast
- Dashed hand-drawn route lines London / Leith -> Black River with moving ship dots
- Whip pans (motion-smeared) between desk and map scenes, zoom transitions between map scales
- Lower subtitles from word timings (faster-whisper); synthesized documentary bed (pad + 16th ticks + pulse bass, 92 BPM, one bar grid), paper/marker/pin/whoosh SFX

Files: script.txt, voice.py (Kokoro + whisper timings), render.js, mix.py, build.sh, test_a_map_doc.mp4. Fonts in ../fonts (Caveat OFL, Permanent Marker / Special Elite Apache 2.0).
