In 1822, hundreds of people sailed off to a country... that did not exist.
So here's the thing. It was called Poyais, on the coast of what's now Honduras.
And its ruler? A Scotsman named Gregor MacGregor. Look at this banknote. "Cazique of Poyais." That's him.
His guidebook called Poyais "one of the most healthy and beautiful spots in the world."
It even had a capital, Saint Joseph.
So around two hundred and fifty settlers sailed from London and Leith.
And when they arrived? No capital. No farms. Just jungle.
Fewer than fifty ever made it home. So how do you sell a country... that isn't there?
