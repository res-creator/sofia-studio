In 1822, a man sold land in a country that did not exist.
It was called Poyais.
It had a guidebook. Three hundred fifty-five pages. Which is three hundred fifty-five more pages than the country had.
It had banknotes. Very real paper. Very fake money.
A cobbler was named Shoemaker to the Princess. There was no princess. There was also no country.
About two hundred and fifty people sailed there anyway.
The capital? Some old rubble. And jungle.
Fewer than fifty made it home. That part isn't funny.
And MacGregor? A French court acquitted him.
He retired to Venezuela. With a military pension.
Tough break.
