# Depth pass — storyboard (vizavi_motion)

Format kept from the brief: 1080x1920, 30 fps, ~23 s, TikTok safe zones.
Thread: one paper world seen through a moving camera; money flows from food → land → world → HQ → back to the land.

## Planes (shared by all scenes)
| Plane | Parallax | Focus | Shadow (elevation) | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Far | 0.25 | soft blur | flat | torn paper strips, halftone patches |
| Back | 0.4 | sharp | flat | accent disc (sun) |
| Middle-back | 0.62 | light blur | low | pale paper skyline |
| Hero | 1.0 | sharp | normal | restaurant, land, burger, map, diagram |
| Foreground | 1.25–1.9 | strong blur (DOF) | high, wide, soft | fries, coins, bills, receipt, paper clouds |

Light: one source top-left; shadows follow the real scale on screen, larger/softer on higher layers.
Camera: the scene moves plus a slow constant "breath" (±10 px, ±0.35°, ±1.2% zoom) that dies down on the final frame.

## Scenes — options → choice
1. **Hook (0–3.4).** A) fries and a coin in front of the lens fly past the camera as it punches in; B) a tray slides through the frame; C) a burger shadow grows before the impact; D) the skyline jumps on the impact.
   → **A + C**: the foreground flying outward sells the punch-in, and the growing shadow gives the fall weight.
2. **Real estate (3.4–7.4).** A) the pull-back reveals a bill and a receipt coming into frame from outside; B) the skyline sinks down as the land rises; C) a fence in the foreground; D) a plan grid in the background.
   → **A + skyline as a mid plane**: on the pull-back the foreground moves faster than the hero, the skyline slower.
3. **Map (7.4–11.6).** A) paper clouds above the map, sweeping across on the pan; B) a plane on a path; C) a tilted map with perspective; D) a magnifying glass.
   → **A**: clouds give 3 depths over a flat map without hurting the pins.
4. **Diagram (11.6–17).** A) out-of-focus coins/bills at the corners drift against the camera; B) a near receipt with numbers; C) a desk-surface background; D) depth fog.
   → **A**: echoes the money flows and doesn't overlap the paths.
5. **Finale (17–23).** A) the skyline returns behind the hero on the lift; B) a blurred fries box/coin in the corners; C) the camera arcs around; D) confetti.
   → **A + B**: three depths in the final frame; the camera settles for reading.
