# Episode 4 — Gus at the Hotel (V1)

## Обзор
Серия 4 продолжает историю из Мадрида: Sofia и Gus заселяются в отель. ~6:30, 6 фраз урока, объяснение каждой фразы с картинкой, 2 паузы YOUR TURN, повторение и викторина.
Главная шутка: Gus — гусь, поэтому бронь у него записана как «Gus Goose + 20 burgers», он съедает ключ-карту и весь шведский стол, а выезжать не хочет.
Формат: 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps, только английский, уровень A1–A2.

## Фразы урока
1. I have a reservation. /aɪ hæv ə ˌrezəˈveɪʃn/ = I booked a room before (YOUR TURN)
2. Can I have a key card, please? /kæn aɪ hæv ə kiː kɑːd pliːz/ = asking for the room key
3. What time is breakfast? /wɒt taɪm ɪz ˈbrekfəst/ = asking when the meal is
4. What's the Wi-Fi password? /wɒts ðə ˈwaɪ faɪ ˈpɑːswɜːd/ = the secret word for the internet
5. The air conditioning doesn't work. /ði eə ˌkɒndɪˈʃənɪŋ ˈdʌznt wɜːk/ = it is hot, the machine is broken (YOUR TURN)
6. What time is check-out? /wɒt taɪm ɪz ˈtʃek aʊt/ = when you leave the hotel

## Сценарий
Реплики на английском, в [скобках] — теги эмоций ElevenLabs, курсивом (*...*) — что на экране. e1–e6 — объяснения Sofia с картинками.

### 1. Приезд: вращающаяся дверь
*У входа в Hotel Pato Real. Sofia с чемоданом, Gus входит во вращающуюся дверь.*
l01 SOFIA [excited] We are in Madrid! And this is our hotel.
l02 GUS [deadpan] It has a spinning door. I like it.
*Гусь крутится в двери круг за кругом.*
l03 SOFIA [laughing] Gus! Please stop spinning!
l04 GUS [dizzy] I... cannot... stop.
*Титр: GUS AT THE HOTEL.*

### 2. Стойка регистрации
*Лобби, стойка, за ней Carmen. На стойке колокольчик.*
l05 CARMEN [warm] Good evening! Welcome to Hotel Pato Real. How can I help you?
l06 SOFIA [politely] Hello! I have a reservation.   (карточка NEW PHRASE)
e1 SOFIA [friendly] A reservation means you booked a room before.
*Картинка RESERVATION. YOUR TURN 1: I have a reservation.*
l07 CARMEN [friendly] Of course. What is your name, please?
l08 SOFIA [cheerful] Sofia. And my friend is Gus.
l09 CARMEN [typing] ...Yes. A room for two nights. And... one goose?

*Крупно: бронь «Gus Goose + 20 burgers».*
l11 CARMEN [confused] The booking says: Gus Goose, plus twenty burgers.
l12 SOFIA [embarrassed] He... travels with his food.
l13 CARMEN [smiling] No problem! Welcome to Madrid.
l14 SOFIA [politely] Can I have a key card, please?   (карточка NEW PHRASE)
e2 SOFIA [friendly] A key card opens the door of your room.
l15 CARMEN [warm] Here you are. Two key cards. Room four-oh-four.
*Gus берёт карту клювом и проглатывает.*
l16 SOFIA [shocked] Gus! Did you eat the key card?!
l17 GUS [deadpan] It was crunchy.
l18 CARMEN [sighs] ...Here is another one.
l19 SOFIA [curious] What time is breakfast?   (карточка NEW PHRASE)

l20 CARMEN [cheerful] Breakfast is from seven to ten, in the restaurant.
l21 GUS [hopeful] Seven... in the morning?
l22 CARMEN [smiling] Yes. Seven in the morning.
l23 GUS [deadpan] I will be there at six.

### 3. Лифт и коридор
*Лифт. Bellboy Pablo с тележкой везёт чемоданы. Gus с подозрением смотрит на кнопки.*
l24 PABLO [friendly] Follow me, please! The lift is here.
l25 GUS [curious] A lift? Is it a flying machine?
l26 SOFIA [laughing] No, Gus. A lift is an elevator. It goes up.

### 4. Номер: жара и Wi-Fi
*Номер 404, вечер. Pablo ставит чемоданы. В номере жарко: термометр красный.*
l27 SOFIA [happy] What a beautiful room!
l28 GUS [flatly] It is very hot in here.
l29 SOFIA [concerned] Oh no. The air conditioning doesn't work.   (карточка NEW PHRASE)
e5 SOFIA [friendly] Air conditioning is the machine that makes the room cool.
*Картинки HOT / COLD. YOUR TURN 2: The air conditioning doesn't work.*
l30 GUS [deadpan] I can fix it.
*Gus машет крыльями, как вентилятор. Термометр падает: 30 → 25.*
l31 SOFIA [laughing] Gus is not an air conditioner!
l32 PABLO [apologetic] Sorry, sorry! One minute... Done! Now it is cool.
l33 SOFIA [curious] Thank you! What's the Wi-Fi password?   (карточка NEW PHRASE)
e4 SOFIA [friendly] A password is a secret word. You need it for the internet.
l34 PABLO [friendly] It is: burgers, one, two, three.
l35 GUS [innocent] That is my password too.

### 5. Завтрак
*Утро, часы 6:00. Gus уже стоит перед закрытой дверью ресторана, Sofia сонная.*
l36 SOFIA [yawns] Gus... it is six o'clock. Breakfast is at seven.
l37 GUS [deadpan] I know. I am early.
*Часы быстро крутятся до 7:00. Carmen открывает дверь. Gus проносится по шведскому столу, стол пустеет.*
l38 SOFIA [shocked] Gus! You ate the whole buffet!
l39 CARMEN [exasperated] Sir... that was breakfast for fifty guests.
l40 GUS [satisfied] It was a good breakfast.

### 6. Выезд

l41 SOFIA [politely] Excuse me. What time is check-out?   (карточка NEW PHRASE)
e6 SOFIA [friendly] Check-out is when you leave the hotel and give back the key.
l42 CARMEN [friendly] Check-out is at eleven o'clock.
l43 GUS [alarmed] Eleven? Today?

l45 GUS [deadpan] Then I will live in the lobby.
*Carmen даёт счёт: «1 key card, 50 breakfasts, 20 burgers».*
l46 CARMEN [smiling] Or you can pay the bill, sir.
l47 GUS [calmly] ...I will check out.
