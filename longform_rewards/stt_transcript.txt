Nobody wanted piles of dead rats at the police station. It worked on paper. At the peak, over 20,000 tails came in a single day. Then officials started seeing rats running around with no tails. People had figured it out. Cut off the tail, let the rat go, let it make more rats and more tails. Inspectors even found rat farms outside the city. The city wanted fewer rats.
