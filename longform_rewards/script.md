# How It's Run — long-form (YouTube, 16:9) — "When Paying People Backfired"
Draft for the owner's approval. ~1,050 spoken words ≈ 6–7 min at Chris's pace (about 3.1 words/s + pauses). American English.
One idea through the whole video: **pay for a number, and people will deliver the number — not the thing you wanted.**
Chapters reuse the checked facts of the shorts (emu_ep12, rats_ep26, hoover_ep19, pudding_ep18) + one new chapter (Wells Fargo).
