NOT USED
- No Leslie Jones photo from the Boston Public Library / Digital Commonwealth collections was used.
- Firehouse 31 (storyboard item 7): no public-domain photo that is clearly identified as Engine 31's
  firehouse was found on Commons, so it was skipped rather than mislabelled. The Library of Congress
  photo "Boston, Mass. Boston molasses explosion" (American Red Cross, loc.gov item 2017679979) could
  not be downloaded (rate limits) and its contents are not described, so it was not used either.
- No stock video. No AI-generated images.

AUDIO
=====================================================================
- Narration (v2): ElevenLabs, voice "Bryan" (bPMKpgEe88vKSwusXTMU, added to the account from the Voice Library), model eleven_v4,
  the script read word for word (vo/script.txt). eleven_v4 ignored the speed setting, so the recording was sped up
  to 1.12x with rubberband (pitch and formants preserved). Short 0.3–0.5 s beats after "splits apart", "owned the tank?"
  and "anarchists." (tools/timeline.py). v1 voice "Z" kept in vo/v1_z/.
- Narration (final_todd.mp4): ElevenLabs, voice "Todd - Clear, Engaging and Educational" (g14YnDYCsy3k7XLlcKlO,
  added to the account from the Voice Library), model eleven_v4, same script word for word, natural speed:
  no time-stretch. One 0.8 s beat after "splits apart." (Todd's own pauses between sentences are up to 0.88 s).
  Files and settings: vo/todd/ (config.json).
- Music and all sound effects (wind, metal creaks, splitting steel, low rumble, crowd murmur) were
  synthesised by code in tools/sound.py. No samples or third-party recordings → no copyright claims.
