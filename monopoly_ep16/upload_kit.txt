DESCRIPTION (TikTok / Shorts)
The man in charge of keeping McDonald's Monopoly fair was the one rigging it. 🍟 Jerome Jacobson ran security at the company that handled the game pieces, pulled the winners and handed them to a network of friends and family. The FBI found out, and the arrests began in 2001. Scenes are a re-enactment; the case is real.
