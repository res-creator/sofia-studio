## Storyboard (actions, no cards)
1. Hook: a McDonald's-style restaurant window, a game piece peels off a sheet; the camera pulls to a man in a security badge sliding it into his pocket (s01–s02).
2. Print room: Jacobson at a table of piece sheets, holds one to the light, nods; pulls winners out (s03–s04).
3. Handoff: he passes envelopes to friends/family in a parking lot; they cash in at a counter, big-prize pieces stack up (s05–s07).
4. FBI: two agents in a van; phone call with a McDonald's executive who agrees to keep the game running (s08–s10).
5. Arrests: doors, cuffs, a wall of faces (s11–s12). Court: gavel, Jacobson hears his sentence (s13).
6. Ending: the empty security desk, a single game piece on it (echoes the first frame) (s14).
Rules: re-enactment, generic figures, no logos (brand name as text only), no invented quotes.
