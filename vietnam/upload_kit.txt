Is Vietnam really cheap? One day in Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon), 5 a.m. to dawn: $1 coffee, banh mi under $1 — and a family of four in one 5 m² room.

This 4K travel documentary asks what Vietnam's low prices really cost. Average pay is about $350 a month. Housing analysts say a Ho Chi Minh City resident needs 53 years' income to buy a home. A family of four rents a room of about 2 x 2.5 m (roughly 54 sq ft). And in the sea of motorbikes, about 30 people died on Vietnam's roads every day in 2025.

Along the way: Ben Thanh market and the tourist "first price", the beaches of Nha Trang and Da Nang, the Golden Bridge at Ba Na Hills, and Bui Vien Street after dark. The film also touches on Hanoi, Hoi An, the Cu Chi tunnels, the War Remnants Museum and Agent Orange, and the Mekong Delta's Cai Rang floating market in Can Tho.
