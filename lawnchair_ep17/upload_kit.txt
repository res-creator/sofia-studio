DESCRIPTION (TikTok / Shorts)
Two airline pilots spotted a man sitting in a lawn chair at 16,000 feet 🎈 In 1982, Larry Walters tied 42 helium balloons to a chair in his California backyard. He expected a gentle ride. Instead he shot nearly three miles up, popped balloons with a pellet gun, dropped the gun, dumped water ballast to land, and snagged power lines on the way down. The FAA fined him $1,500. That same lawn chair is now in the Smithsonian's collection. Scenes are a re-enactment; the story is real.
Sources: Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum collection (Larry Walters' lawn chair, object A20181388000); Smithsonian Air & Space magazine, "Flying Lawn Chair"; Los Angeles Times archive, Nov. 24, 1993.
