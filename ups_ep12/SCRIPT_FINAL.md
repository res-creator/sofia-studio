## What was fixed and why

1. **s13 — ORION → "UPS's route math".** The only source for the 20 seconds (CNN, Feb 2017, quoting UPS's Jack Levis) says
   "The software can give an undesirable left turn a penalty that adds 20 seconds to the estimated route time" — it says "the software"
   and does not tie this sentence to ORION by name. "UPS's route math" is exactly what the source supports. "Get a penalty in" is also
   more natural than "add a penalty to … math".
2. **s20 — "And every mile counts."** Right after "the right-turn route was LONGER", the $50 million line could be heard as
   "right turns save $50 million" (against rule 5). The bridge makes it clear that miles cost money too — which is why lefts are not banned.
3. **s21 — "But" → "So".** Avoids two "But" lines in a row and links the ban line to the logic of s20.
