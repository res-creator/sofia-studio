PINNED COMMENT
Sources: UC Davis News (2011): David Phillips, a UC Davis civil engineer, found Healthy Choice pudding cups for 25 cents each, spent about $3,140 and earned 1.25 million miles; UC Davis (2003) and the Honolulu Star-Bulletin (2002): about 12,000 / 12,150 cups, the story inspired the film Punch-Drunk Love. Promotion terms (500 miles per 10 barcodes, doubled if mailed early), the Salvation Army volunteers and the tax write-off (reported as $815) are from the Wikipedia article on Phillips, citing Dateline UC Davis (2000) and Snopes. Accounts differ on the exact cup count (12,150 vs 12,560). "About 100 miles per cup" is our math (1,253,000 ÷ ~12,150). The tax write-off did not cover the cost of the pudding. YOUTUBE SHORTS
Title: He Bought 12,000 Puddings for a Million Airline Miles (true story)
Description: In 1999, UC Davis engineer David Phillips spotted a Healthy Choice promotion: mail in barcodes, get free airline miles (reportedly 500 miles per 10 barcodes, doubled for early mail-ins). The cheapest barcode in the store was a 25-cent pudding cup. He bought over 12,000 of them, Salvation Army volunteers peeled the barcodes, and the pudding was donated to people in need. About $3,140 of pudding turned into 1,253,000 frequent-flyer miles, and his story inspired the pudding plan in Adam Sandler's film Punch-Drunk Love (2002). He wasn't buying dessert — he was buying a million miles. (Illustrated; generic characters.)
