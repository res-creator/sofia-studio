=== PINNED COMMENT ===
Quick facts 📌 This was DoorDash's pay model from 2017 to 2019 — it changed in 2019, so it's not how pay works today. The $10 guarantee in the video is an illustrative example. DoorDash always paid at least $1, and if a tip was bigger than the guarantee, the driver earned more. In 2025 DoorDash agreed to a $16.75 million settlement with the New York Attorney General over this pay model.
Sources: New York Attorney General press release (2025) — ag.ny.gov · DoorDash Dasher pay page — dasher.doordash.com/en-us/about/pay
