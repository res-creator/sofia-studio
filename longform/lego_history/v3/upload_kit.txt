DESCRIPTION
By 2004, LEGO had invented thousands of new pieces — and most of them were used just once. That year the world's most famous toy company lost almost two billion Danish kroner. This is the story of how LEGO grew too big for its own system, nearly went under, and saved itself by going back to its oldest idea: fewer pieces, used again and again.
