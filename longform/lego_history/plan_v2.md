## Visual storytelling (images carry it; text kept to a minimum)

**Recurring device: the toy town.** One code-built table-top town of bricks (from 2–3 Figma prop sheets of generic bricks, no logo) that **changes with the story**: neat and modular (ch 1–2) → overgrown with odd one-off pieces, wobbly towers, a theme park and a TV screen stuck on (ch 3) → lights go out, empty shelves (ch 4) → cleared back to a tidy grid (ch 5) → the clean town plus the 1958 brick in close-up (ch 7). The viewer can *see* complexity grow and shrink without a chart.
