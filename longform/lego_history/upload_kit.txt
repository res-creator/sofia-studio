DESCRIPTION
By 2004, LEGO had invented thousands of new pieces — and most of them were used in exactly one set. That same year the company lost almost two billion Danish kroner. This is the story of how the world's favorite toy nearly sank its own maker: the system that made LEGO great, the "beyond the brick" years, the year without a movie, the outsider who took over at 35, the outsourcing mistake inside the rescue — and the 1958 brick that still clicks today.
