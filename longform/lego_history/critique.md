# Self-critique of script v1 — where viewers may leave, and what to improve

Overall: the story is true, complex and well-sourced, with one clear arc (the brick → "beyond the brick" → back to the brick) and a strong circular payoff.
Main risk: the **central character arrives late** (Knudstorp at 7:35) and the first half is history + context with no single protagonist.

## Retention risks (by time)
| When | Risk | Fix (proposal) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | "Counting pieces" is intriguing but less visceral than money or a person in trouble. | Open on the image + money in the first line: "In 2004, LEGO lost almost two billion kroner — and one reason was sitting on its shelves: more than twelve thousand different pieces." Test both versions as cold-open stills. |
| 0:55–2:45 | Classic founder biography — exactly what the brief said to avoid; 2 minutes before the main conflict returns. | Keep it tight and dramatic (two fires, the insane machine bet, the brick that wouldn't stick). Cut c2 (name) to one sentence, end every beat on a turn. Target 1:30 instead of 1:50. |
| 2:45–4:05 | Market forces (patents, retailers, China, currency) can feel like a list. | Show each as one physical gag (copies elbowing in, the krone seesaw, the superstore cart) — already in the storyboard. Keep the narration to one sentence each. |
| 4:05–6:05 | Strongest chapter (Galidor, piece explosion). Risk: too many product names. | Say only Explore, Jack Stone, Galidor, Bionicle; no extra names. |

| 10:10–11:20 | Numbers parade (movie, Mattel, buy-back, 2025 revenue). | Keep two numbers max; let pictures carry the rest. The final two lines are the emotional end — give them silence and the brick click. |

## Content risks
- **No direct human voices.** Only two quotes (CFO, COO). The story would gain from 1–2 verified public quotes by Knudstorp or Kjeld Kirk Kristiansen from interviews
  (to be verified word-for-word before use; the popular "burning platform" line has inconsistent wording/dates — not used).
- **h4 "had never run a company"** — true in the sense of never having been a CEO; safer wording: "who had never been a CEO."
- **"Kjeld took the controls himself" (y4)** — Jan 2004 announcement says he took day-to-day responsibility as CEO; OK, but keep "took the controls" as is (accurate).
- **Colour count (b7)** — sources differ (≈ 100–110 in 2004); keep "more than a hundred."
- **Element counts** — two sources (14,200 inventory in 2004 vs 12,900 → 7,000 in the rescue). Script uses "more than twelve thousand" (cold open), "more than fourteen thousand" (b5) and "roughly thirteen thousand" (o4) — consistent with the sources but could confuse viewers. **Proposal:** use one number everywhere: "more than twelve thousand" … "cut to about seven thousand."
- **Trademarks:** minifigures, Star Wars, Harry Potter, Bionicle, Galidor must not be drawn as their real designs; generic shapes + plain-text names only. Thumbnail without the LEGO logo.
- **Tone:** no motivational ending — the last line is concrete ("A brick from 1958… that still fits"). Keep it.

## Length
1,529 spoken words ≈ 10:45–11:30 finished. With the "plant Knudstorp" line and a tighter chapter 1, still ≈ 11 min — inside the 10–12 min target and over 8 min for mid-roll ads.
