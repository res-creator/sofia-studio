**Overall: 8 / 10.** Clearly stronger than v1 (hook, chain, Knudstorp planted, one number set, ~1 min shorter), but not a "10": chapter 1 opens with biography and chapter 5 still has a run of steps.

## Weak spots fixed before submission
1. "Fifty years later" (wrong: 1960 → 2000 is ~40) → "Four decades later".
2. "Designers had been told to think beyond the brick. So they did what designers do" (an interpretation shown as fact) → "Designers were free to invent. And much of what they invented was new pieces."
3. The 1954 buyer story was given as plain fact → "According to LEGO's own history…".
4. "Bought the Legoland parks back" (loose: the 2019 deal bought Merlin) → "led a deal to buy Merlin, the company that runs the Legoland parks".
5. o5 was a bare list item → chained to o4 ("With fewer pieces, the sets went back…").
6. A prop label used a set number that could be a real product → "ONE SET ONLY".
