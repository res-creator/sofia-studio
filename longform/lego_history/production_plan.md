## 2. Visual concept by chapter (shot ideas; humour in *italics*)
**Look:** approved Figma painted-cartoon style; each era has its own light: 1930s honey-wood workshop; 1940s–50s cool factory light; 1980s warm family living room;
1990s fluorescent toy superstore; 2003 cold blue boardroom at night; 2005 morning light; 2014 cinema glow; today bright Billund daylight. Brick colours (red/yellow/blue)
are the accent thread through every era. **Trademark care:** generic little figures (not copies of the minifigure), no LEGO logo; licensed worlds (Star Wars, Harry Potter,
Bionicle, Galidor) shown only as generic boxes/silhouettes with plain-text names — never their characters.

**0 Cold open**
- Endless warehouse of shelves, each with ONE odd piece [F plate "piece warehouse"]; camera flies down an aisle [C]; a clerk with a clipboard counts, the counter spins past 12,000 [C type].
- *A single tiny bin labelled "used once" falls off a shelf and starts an avalanche of pieces* [C, prop sheet bricks].
- Red ledger, kroner coins draining from a giant brick-shaped piggy bank [R wooden-toy/props sheet + C].
- Silhouette of a young man in a suit at the office door, backlit [F plate boardroom + puppet "Outsider"].
- Hard cut to black → "1932" [C].

**1 The Carpenter**
- Billund 1932, snowy village street, carpenter's shop [F plate + extras sheet villagers]; Ole Kirk puppet at his bench, no customers, empty furniture [F plate workshop].
- He carves a wooden duck → *duck rolls off the bench by itself, wheels squeaking* [prop sheet wooden toys, C].
- "leg godt" letters assemble from wooden blocks [C].
- 1942 night fire: workshop plate re-graded red + code fire glow, embers, smoke [R+C]; morning: Ole Kirk with a hammer, rebuilding (shrug + determined) [puppet].
- 1947 factory: giant green injection machine [F plate factory]; *coins pour into the machine's hopper; bricks drop out one by one* [C].
- 1949 bricks stack and *wobble and topple* [C]; Godtfred puppet at a drafting desk 1958 [F plate desk], pencil draws tubes [C line drawing], click — they lock, sparkle [C]; patent stamp "28 JAN 1958 · 13:58" [C].
- 1960 warehouse of wooden toys burns [F plate wooden-toy warehouse + C fire]; next shot the same aisle, now plastic bricks [C swap].
- "System of play": a toy city assembles itself across a tabletop [F plate toy city + C pieces dropping in]; a 1958 brick clicks onto a 2025 brick (macro) [C].

**2 The Golden Years**
- 1978 living room, kids on the carpet building a town [F plate living room + extras kids]; little generic figures hop onto the town [C].
- Growth = *the toy city grows taller every 5 years, buildings stacking up like a chart* (no graph) [R toy city + C].
- Patent papers flutter away; copies appear: *a row of look-alike bricks in slightly wrong colours elbowing in* [C]; courtroom silhouette, gavel [extras or C].
- World changes: kid turns from bricks to a glowing TV/console [R living room + C]; toy superstore aisle with a giant shopping cart [F plate superstore]; currency: *Danish krone coin on a seesaw outweighing a dollar* [C].
- 1993–98: product boxes multiply on the shelf until it bows [R superstore + C]; 1998 boardroom, red number, the Owner puppet rubs his forehead [F plate boardroom + puppet "Owner"].

**3 Beyond the Brick**
- The turnaround manager (secondary sprite) unrolls a map "BEYOND THE BRICK" [C type]; *the brick on the table gets pushed off the edge* [C].
- Montage of expansions, each a quick whip: theme-park gate [F plate park], clothes rack, game controller, TV [prop sheet 2000s], movie-reel boxes with plain titles [C].
- Explore / Jack Stone / Galidor as generic boxes; *a chunky hero figure snaps together in 3 seconds and then just stands there; a kid yawns* [extras kid + props].
- Design studio: designers inventing pieces, *a conveyor of new shapes, each getting its own steel mold that drops with a clang* [F plate design studio + props molds].
- Warehouse callback (b7b) [R cold-open plate]; colour swatches fan out to 100+ [C].
- Blockbuster cover: *a movie poster silhouette held up in front of a crumbling wall like a curtain* [C on R superstore].

**4 The Year Without a Movie**
- Cinema marquee with empty letters, "2003" [R cinema plate or C]; store shelf of movie sets emptying, buyer crossing out orders [F plate retailer back room + extras buyer].
- Fixed costs: *factory machines keep running at full speed into an empty warehouse; boxes pile to the ceiling* [R factory + C].
- Boardroom at night [R boardroom, cold grade]: record loss stamp [C]; Owner puppet takes the chair himself.
- 60/40: *a giant pie made of bricks; 60 % slice glows, the other 40 % turns grey and crumbles* — no axis/graph [C].
- "Will LEGO survive?" — the Billund factory sign flickering at night, rain [R plate + C rain].

**5 The Outsider**
- Morning, door opens: the Outsider puppet steps in; *the family portraits on the wall turn their eyes to him* [R boardroom + C].
- "Manage for cash": he pulls the plug on a leaking pipe of coins [C].
- Legoland gate with a SOLD sign; the gate rolls off on a truck [R park plate + C].
- *Pieces vacuumed off the design-studio shelves; half the bins fly into a skip* [R studio + C].
- Back to basics: police station, fire truck, race car built on the table [R toy city + props].
- Retailer meeting: buyer puppet/extra nods [R back room].
- Fan convention hall full of adult builders and huge brick models [F plate convention + extras fans]; the Outsider walks in, fans turn [puppet].
- 2005 profit: sun rises over Billund, the sign lights green [R + C].

**6 The Mistake Inside the Rescue**
- Trucks carry molds to a big modern contract factory [F plate modern factory]; molds arrive before machines — *a worker stares at a pallet of molds in an empty hall* [extras].
- Parts shortage: a set box with a missing piece-shaped hole [C].
- 2008: the trucks drive back [R + C reverse].

**7 The Brick**
- Sales "quarter a year": the toy city grows again, brighter [R toy city].
- 2014 cinema, audience faces lit by the screen [F plate cinema + extras]; *a brick-built trophy taller than the Mattel-style doll box next to it (generic)* [C].

- 2025: modern Billund in daylight [F plate modern Billund].
- Recap (a5): six quick callbacks — patent paper, TV, superstore, krone seesaw, empty cinema, the piece warehouse [R everything].
- Final: Ole Kirk's workshop bench; a 1958 brick and a 2025 brick click together; camera pulls back [R workshop + C]. End card.

### Animation complexity
- Puppet acting: 4 rigged characters × ~25 acting beats — medium (rig exists; per character ~1 h to key/measure/test).
- Hardest shots: self-assembling toy city (many bricks dropping on correct studs), fires (glow + smoke + embers), conveyor of molds, piece avalanche — all code FX, medium.
- Everything else: camera + sprite moves, already in `figmakit/lib/scene.js`.

### Production time (after script approval)
1. Shot list + asset list per line: 2–3 h. 2. Figma generation (40) + download + keying + 4 character configs: ~1 day (most of it rig fitting and checks).
3. Voice: ~1 h (when ElevenLabs quota is available). 4. Animation, 7 chapters in parallel (4 agents): ~1–1.5 days incl. stills checks and one render.
5. Sound mix, −14 LUFS, SRT, chapters, thumbnail, upload kit, QC: ~0.5 day. **≈ 3 working days.**

### Re-use without losing quality
- One boardroom for three eras (grade: sepia 1998, cold blue 2003, warm morning 2004) + different props on the table.
- The toy-city plate is the film's "map": it grows, empties and regrows — the same plate with code layers on top.
- Workshop plate returns at the end for the payoff (circle structure).
- Library: `figmakit` already has a 1899 office and Candler (not usable here — wrong era/people), but its code (camera, FX, puppets) is reused 100 %.
- New assets go into `figmakit/assets/` after the film (Ole Kirk, workshop, toy city, boardroom) — reusable for future toy/business stories.
