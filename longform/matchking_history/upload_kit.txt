DESCRIPTION
In the 1920s, a Swedish engineer named Ivar Kreuger made about two thirds of the world's matches and lent nearly $400 million to some fifteen governments. Banks trusted him. Millions of investors bought his securities. Then, in March 1932, he was found dead in Paris — and when accountants opened his books, about $250 million of his assets had never existed.
This is the story of the Match King: a real business, a brilliant idea about money, and one of the biggest frauds of the 20th century.
