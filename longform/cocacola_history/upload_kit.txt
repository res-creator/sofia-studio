DESCRIPTION
In 1886, a pharmacist in Atlanta sold about nine glasses a day of a new syrup drink. Today, people drink about 2.2 billion servings of the company's drinks every single day.
So how did it happen? A wounded Civil War veteran and his kettle. A salesman who gave the drink away. A bottling deal sold for one dollar. A bottle you could recognize in the dark. A promise to every soldier in World War II. A rival that wouldn't quit — and New Coke, the 79 days America said no.
Animated history by How It's Run. Illustrated re-enactment; historical figures drawn from period portraits, everyone else is generic. Not affiliated with or sponsored by The Coca-Cola Company or PepsiCo.
