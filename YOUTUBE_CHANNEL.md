# YouTube channel: Known in Advance (@KnownInAdvance)

## Description (YouTube "About")
Real disasters, told through what was known before they happened.

Known in Advance is a documentary channel about modern catastrophes: volcanic eruptions, wildfires, floods, collapses. Each film follows one event in order: the place, the people, the first signs, the decisions made with the knowledge of the time, and the moment everything changed.

We work from primary sources: archive footage, original interviews, eyewitness voices, official reports and real maps. We show what was known, what was not, and what changed afterward.

New documentaries regularly. Subscribe to follow the next story.
