## Narration rules for the Short
- Say "according to…" or "the story goes" for: the whole Eiffel Tower con (fake official, Crillon meeting, Poisson, the money, the bribe, the second attempt). Good wording: "According to a Secret Service agent's memoir…"
- Don't give the amount as a fact. Use "a fortune", or "one account says $70,000".
- Say "sold it once, then tried again". Don't claim the second sale went through: in the standard version the second buyer went to the police and Lustig fled to America.
- Facts you can state plainly: the 1889 temporary tower and the 20-year plan; the counterfeiting; arrest 10 May 1935; Alcatraz; death on 11 Mar 1947 in Springfield, Missouri (not on Alcatraz).
- The "Ten Commandments" are "attributed to" Lustig.
