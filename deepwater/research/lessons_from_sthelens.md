# Lessons from the Mount St. Helens film, applied to Deepwater Horizon

The owner and the critic both found problems in the St. Helens cut. Each one comes with a rule for this film.

- **4:39–5:42 felt like slides.**
  - *Problem:* document → text card → document → document → map.
  - *Rule:* never run more than two "static explanation" items (document, card, diagram) in a row. Each run must be broken by real action or environment footage, or by a person speaking.
- **People came in late.**
  - *Problem:* Johnston was introduced early, but other people only appeared when they died.
  - *Rule:* introduce each key person by role before their action matters, with a real image or their own voice if available. With no image, use a lower third on relevant footage, never a full-screen text card.
- **Technical exposition came in blocks.**
  - *Problem:* the 1978 report and the bulge were explained up front.
  - *Rule:* explain a term at the moment the story needs it, one term per beat. Show the concept on the same schematic every time.
- **Topic-filler shots** (the logs under the Colombia line).
  - *Rule:* every shot must show the event being described, or be labelled as a different time or place with a reason. Where this cannot be done, use a labelled schematic or document instead.
- **Ambiguous dates and later footage.**
  - *Rule:* each archive clip carries its date and time where known. Night footage of April 20–21 and daytime footage of April 21–22 must never be presented as the first minutes of the blowout.
- **Double name supers, labels cut off at the frame edge, and white text on bright images.**
  - *Rule:* run the QC checklist on rendered stills of every map, label and lower third. The film's own burned-in names count as name supers.
- **Wording the facts.**
  - *Rule:* use the exact primary-source figure or range, never a merge of two sources. For anything contested, use the investigators' own wording.
- **Rights.**
  - *Rule:* check music inside official films separately, and check uncredited prints. Note each risk in `materials.md`.

## What worked and should be kept
- A cold open on one concrete, documented situation, then a jump back in time.
- Original voices (interviews and testimony) given room, with music dipped under them.
- One consistent schematic that adds a layer each time it returns, labelled "RECONSTRUCTION · SIMPLIFIED, NOT TO SCALE".
- Documents shown in context first, then one line highlighted.
- The same voice and settings: Todd, `eleven_v4`, stability 0.45, similarity 0.8, style 0.35, speaker boost.
