# Full film — preliminary block plan (to be refined after the owner reviews the opening)

**Central question:** why was the well judged safe, and why didn't the emergency protection stop the blowout? Every block separates four things: known risks, observed signs, how the people involved read them at the time, and the investigators' conclusions. Sources: `facts.md`. Materials: `materials.md`.
