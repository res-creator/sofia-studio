# Deepwater Horizon: archive materials log

Researched 2026-11-03. Status: **L** = link only, **D** = downloaded, **V** = downloaded and viewed (contact sheet made with ffmpeg and checked by eye).
Raw files are in `deepwater/assets/raw/` (about 450 MB in total).

**General rights notes**
- Every DVIDS page below shows the label **"PUBLIC DOMAIN"** in its rights box. DVIDS also states: "This work, [title], must comply with the restrictions shown on https://www.dvidshub.net/about/copyright."
- All DVIDS items come from the U.S. Coast Guard (USCG Heartland, Atlantic Area, Headquarters and 8th District External Affairs). Those videos are SD (640x480 or 720x406 at about 800k), and that is the best public file. The originals need a DVIDS login: `/download/...` returns HTTP 403 without one. Photos were taken as the public `2000w_q95.jpg` renditions; DVIDS lists the originals at about 3000–4300 px.
- In the videos, audio is silent over the title slates and present over the footage. So the sound is the original field/aircraft sound, not added music. I did not listen to it.
- Wikimedia Commons and Wikipedia returned **HTTP 429** for every request, so I did not retry them. history.uscg.mil returned **403** (bot protection), so I did not try to get around it. The DVIDS site search returned 202 (a challenge page), so I found items by stepping through neighbouring IDs on the pages themselves.

## 2. The rig before the accident

- **No public-domain pre-2010 image of Deepwater Horizon itself was found.** Search turned up only post-explosion federal images. The Wikipedia lead photo "File:Deepwater Horizon.jpg" is a Transocean photo of the rig drilling Tiber (2009), used on en.wikipedia under a non-free/fair-use claim, so it is **not reusable** (L, not downloaded). I could not check Commons directly because of the 429 errors.
- MMS/BOEM inspection photos: none online found. The USCG JIT report (Vol. I, April 2011) and the BOEMRE report (Sept 2011) reproduce rig photos credited to Transocean, so they are third-party and copyrighted. L only.
- **The best legal stand-in** is DVIDS 1095904 "Deepwater Horizon crewmember diagram": the rig deck plan used at the JIT hearing, with a push-pin labelled "MURRAY" (chief electrician Chad Murray) showing where he was at the time of the blowout. Kenner, May 2010. https://www.dvidshub.net/image/1095904 · **V** `raw/dvids_photos/dvids_img_1095904.jpg`
- Sister rigs at the site in operation (to show what a rig like this looks like at work, not DWH itself): DVIDS 1095288 Development Driller III at dusk, May 7, 2010 (PO3 Patrick Kelley) **V** `dvids_img_1095288.jpg`; 1095659 DD-III, Q4000 and Discoverer Enterprise, May 18, 2010 **V** `dvids_img_1095659.jpg`.
