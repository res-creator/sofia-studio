## Principle
Orientation → move in to the part that matters → the action or change → a visible result → transition. One visual system across the film: steel = structure, amber = blind shear ram/BOP, light grey = drill pipe, brown = mud, blue = seawater, red-orange = oil and gas; cyan outline = "how it was meant to work". Inactive parts dimmed. Real equipment first, then its schematic.
