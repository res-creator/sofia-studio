## Facts behind the narration (all in `research/verification.md` or `research/chronology.md`)
- Advisory 21 (10 a.m. Aug 25) first "catastrophic"; Advisory 22 (4 p.m.) "15 to 30 inches … 40 inches"; WPC Day-3 High Risk "unprecedented" (NWS SA p. 38).
- Greene: Friday flights cancelled, waiting to send a crew; "follow roads", 911 addresses, Google Maps on phones; landing in ballparks and car parks during bands (620838, his own words).
- Emmett's reasons: NPR transcript, Aug 28. Rita: NTSB HAR-07/01 (23 in the bus fire, Sept 23, 2005; landfall Sept 24); "dozens" instead of the secondary "73".
- Landfall ≈ 10 p.m. Aug 25 near Rockport, Category 4 (NHC TCR). First flash flood emergency 9:27 p.m. Aug 26 (SA p. 8). 3.08 in in 30 min (HGX discussion 10:17 p.m.). SA p. 7, exact scope: "By Sunday morning, August 27, 48-hour rainfall amounts were approaching 25 inches in the Houston area, with most of that rain falling in a 9- to 12-hour time frame late Saturday night and early Sunday morning" — this is the first 48 h in the Houston area, not Harvey's total (Houston metro storm totals reached 36–48 in by Aug 30). The finale says only that the downpour hit Houston on Saturday night.
