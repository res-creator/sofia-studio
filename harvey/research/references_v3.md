# Reference films — observations for Harvey v3 (Nov 5, 2026)

**How these were studied.** I did not watch either film with sound. For each film:
- I took frames every 6 seconds and read them as contact sheets;
- I ran an automatic speech transcript locally (faster-whisper small.en, on this machine, no paid service).

Timecodes are file time of the copies the owner uploaded (320×180). Notes on sound, pace inside a shot and music are therefore limited to what the transcript and frame order show. Nothing here is taken into our film. Their footage is TV/stock material, not free archive.

## A. "The Day That Almost Ended Texas — Harvey 2017" (40:14) — geography, maps, archive + graphics

**Opening (0:00–3:32): one person's night, then the scale, then the question.**
- 0:00–0:06 newspaper headline "Harvey stall, flood rise · Sunday, August 27, 2017" as a date stamp.
- 0:06–2:16 Sgt. Steve Perez told by the narrator over:
  - the police chief's portrait;
  - a night police scene and a radar loop;
  - a barricade at night;
  - **1:48 an aerial of the I-45 / Hardy Toll Road junction with a red circle and a place label**;
  - 2:06 a flooded underpass with a sign;
  - 2:12 a candle vigil.
- Narration (transcript 0:00–2:16) gives him a route, a "truck", a depth of "more than 16 feet" and "more than two hours driving". **These are not in our verified sources. We keep our own verified version (verification.md §12).**
- 2:16–3:32 the scale (68 direct deaths; "a year of Houston's rain in under a week"), then the question "What happens when the rain simply does not stop?" (3:22).

**Maps and graphics (what to learn).**
- One light-grey US/Texas base map that returns again and again:
  - 1:12, 1:18, 1:24 Texas in red with a "36 HOURS" counter;
  - 5:06 "Evacuation warnings";
  - 10:48 "Wind + surge";
  - 26:48 "Heaviest rain: Golden Triangle";
  - 30:12 "USGS stream-gauge network".
  - Each return adds one label.
- Real forecast products shown as documents, then isolated:
  - 0:42 and 6:18 the NWS flood statement page next to its map;
  - 7:42, 8:18–8:24 the WPC 5-day rainfall map with "10″ 20″ 30″" called out;
  - 9:24–9:30 the side-by-side NWS forecast vs observed maps.
- Highlight on real imagery:
  - circles and labels on aerials (1:48 I-45 & Hardy Toll Road; 24:54 "Memorial · West Houston");
  - a pin and label on the Addicks spillway (24:18);
  - "16K cubic feet/second" written over the outlet footage (24:30);
  - the pool extent painted over a satellite image (23:06, 26:12);
  - a cross-section of the dam (22:18 "Section A-A").
- Timeline bars with dates ("48 HOURS", 4:30; "7 WEEKS", 26:30) and bar charts for rain records (14:18–14:30).
- Labels laid onto footage to name what matters:
  - 12:18 "HOME / FLOODED LOW ROAD";
  - 23:42 "RESERVOIR RELEASE / FLOODED BAYOU";
  - 32:54 "RAIN SOAKS IN / FAST RUNOFF".
- Before/after splits with a red arrow (17:24, 38:00, 38:48) and round inset pictures (12:42, 33:12, 39:06).
- Newspaper-style date stamps as chapter markers: 23:12 "Pool 70-yr high · Monday August 28"; 24:00 "Corps begin releases"; 36:54 "Court rules liability".

**Order of explanation.** The reservoir block (21:00–26:30) goes map → spillway aerial → number on the footage → flooded neighbourhood → a resident or reporter → document line (25:24 "Corps says Addicks and Barker dams were not breached"). This is the order the owner asks for: map → footage → person.

**What we do not copy.**
- Stock or TV footage presented as the event.
- Reconstructed details the sources don't give (Perez's route and depth).
- The very dense rhythm of 3–6-second graphics.

## B. "Inside the Hurricane Harvey Aftermath — World's Wildest Weather" (45:38) — witnesses, their own footage, domestic detail

Multi-event episode. Harvey appears at 1:44–6:20 (Corpus Christi/Rockport landfall, storm-chasers), 15:18–20:15, 32:20–36:42 and 42:20–44:58. The other events (tornado, snow, haboob) are irrelevant. We don't copy the structure.

**The Harvey thread is one person, cameraman Michael Melendez, filmed by himself.** Times are from the transcript and frames.
- 16:24–17:30 the threat:
  - aerials of Addicks reservoir;
  - an "Addicks Reservoir" sign (16:36);
  - his mother's interview (Debbie Leo) and his own in a studio.
  - Transcript 16:30–16:58 states the reservoirs "are about to burst" and that "the city orders a controlled release". **Both are wrong for Houston 2017:** the Corps ran the releases, and the courts found the risk of failure minimal. We don't repeat them.
- 17:36–19:30 his night drive filmed from the driver's seat: water across the road, headlights, "it was a river" at an off-ramp (18:56–19:16). The mother's waiting is cut between the shots ("I kept looking at my watch…", 18:28).
- 32:36–36:30, the day after:
  - he parks "eight blocks away" and wades;
  - selfie shots in the water (33:06–33:42);
  - "This is my street" (34:02), "That's my house. My front door is underwater" (34:11–34:16);
  - inside: water in the rooms, ruined printed photos in his hand (34:48–34:54 "I'm wiping my memories away"), the fridge magnets he saved (35:05–35:20);
  - an alligator in the neighbour's yard (35:46).
- 42:24–44:20, ten days later:
  - rescuers with dogs (42:24);
  - debris piles at the kerb (42:30–43:06);
  - inside the gutted house, "the first thing you notice is the smell" (43:14–43:38);
  - ruined home videos "with my dad" (43:54–44:03);
  - epilogue: moved back in April 2018 (44:17).
- Credits (45:06) thank DVIDS-type military sources: Texas National Guard, Air Force, Indiana National Guard, Air National Guard, TSgt Lindsey Maurice. **We found and use the original DVIDS files, not frames from this film.**

**What to learn.**
- The witness's own camera at eye level gives presence. The interview answers what the footage cannot.
- Small domestic objects (photos, magnets, a front door under water) carry the loss better than totals.
- One person is followed across three moments in time: threat, the day after, ten days later.
