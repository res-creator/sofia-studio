# "Harvey: The Storm That Changed Texas Forever" (14:25) — how it works

## What I could and could not check

**What I did, and what that means.**
- I can't listen the way a person does. I analysed the whole sound track as a signal:
  - a word-level transcript (faster-whisper small.en, run locally) with every pause measured;
  - loudness every 0.5 s;
  - sub-bass under 80 Hz (music and impacts; the voice has almost none there);
  - beat regularity, tonal centre and spectral flux (onsets);
  - the level in every gap between words.
- Picture: frames every 2 s across the whole video, plus automatic detection of cuts (305 cuts in 864 s).
- So statements about timing, density, pauses and changes in the music are measured. Statements about the character of the music ("dark", "hopeful") are inferences from those measurements, not from listening.

**What I could not do.**
- The file has one mono channel at 22 kHz, so I could not separate music from archive sound under the voice.
- I can say only where there is *no* voice, and what fills those gaps.

## 1. The opening (0:00–0:53): a hook built as a scene

**Structure.**
- 0:00 a date and clock time on a quiet street: "August 28th, 2017, 9:47 a.m.", with a "09:47" timestamp burned into the shot.
- 0:05–0:23 an ordinary morning in a named subdivision. Pictures: children's photos, a house, children watching TV, a parent with a phone.
- 0:23 the turn: "What none of them knew was that at that exact moment…".
- 0:28–0:45 the danger told as a dilemma: "Open the gates and flood thousands of homes on purpose, or wait and risk an uncontrolled collapse…".
- 0:46 the decision with a second time stamp: "At 9:51 a.m. they started releasing the water."
- 0:50 the sting: "…and nobody had called to warn them."
- 0:53 then it jumps back to landfall. **The question that keeps you watching:** how did it come to this?
- Picture order 0:24–0:52: map pin on the dams → "109% CAPACITY" written on the reservoir → gates and spillway water → control room → "9:51 AM" on the outflow → flooded subdivision from above → title card "WHEN HARVEY STALLED" (0:54–0:58).
- That order is **cause → mechanism → consequence → title**.

**Not usable as a model.** The opening scene is partly invented or wrong:
- the families at breakfast and the weather apps are not sourced;
- the court record has the releases starting around midnight on Aug 28, not at 9:51 a.m.

We keep the device (a precise time, an ordinary place, the approaching threat), not the invention.

**Pace.**

## 4. Voice, music and archive sound together

- The voice runs almost continuously. Gaps between words in the bed measure −26 to −35 dB, against about −28 dB overall. **The music sits only a few dB under the voice** and keeps the momentum.
- I found no passage of archive sound standing on its own. The only gap without voice is the music hit at 0:56. **This film does not use breathing pauses**; its tension comes from writing, pace and the music bed.
- What we take from it:
  - a driving bed under the build-up;
  - short sentences at the peak;
  - a music drop just before a key line.
- What we do differently, following the owner's notes and our rules: we keep a few deliberate pauses where real sound or silence carries meaning.

## 5. Editing: building expectation and showing the result

- **About 20–26 cuts per minute** (shots of 2.5–3 s). The opening runs at 34 per minute.
- **Order inside a beat:** place or number → mechanism → consequence.
  - 0:28–0:52: dam at "109% capacity" → gates → water pouring → control room → flooded homes from above.
  - 1:36–1:44: "50 inches" over a flooded street → "60" → "6 in/hour" over a flooded subdivision → people wading.
  - 4:36–4:46: spillway water → flooded freeway → flooded subdivision.
- Numbers are laid on the footage as large text: "109% capacity", "50 inches", "6 in/hour", "75%", "10,000 homes", "17K rescues", "13K homes", "$2.5 billion".
- Maps and charts appear for one idea at a time:
  - the flood-zone map against the flooded houses (2:42);
  - the Atlas 14 line chart (7:36–7:42);
  - the bar chart of rain totals (7:54).

## 6. What we do not take

- Invented scenes and times: the breakfast scene, "9:47 / 9:51 a.m.".
- Claims that contradict our verified record. Our sources give different figures for each of these:
  - "the dam wasn't failing yet, but if the rain returned it would": the court found the risk minimal;
  - "10,000 homes downstream": about 4,000 downstream and more than 9,000 inside the pools;
  - "three children died in a van in Beaumont": Greens Bayou in Houston, six dead, four of them children;
  - "Rita killed more than 100 in traffic": dozens, 23 of them in one bus fire;
  - "17,000 water rescues": the Coast Guard alone 11,022; Texas more than 122,000.
- TV-station footage, logos and stock imagery used as if it were the event.
- The continuous wall of narration with no pause at all.
