# Hurricane Harvey: people and rights-cleared archive

Researched 2026-11-05. Scope: Houston area and the Texas coast, Aug 25 to Sept 2, 2017.
Rule we followed: use only US federal government works that are marked public domain. We checked the rights label on every item page. We do not use news, AP, Getty, local-TV or social-media material, even when it appears on a .gov page.

Folders:
- Media: `harvey/assets/raw/dvids/dvids_<ID>.mp4`, `harvey/assets/raw/photos/dvids_img_<ID>.jpg` and `harvey/assets/raw/other/`. These are gitignored; `harvey/assets/raw/` is in `.gitignore`.
- Contact sheets: `harvey/research/contact_sheets/<ID>.jpg`, one frame every 3 s (5 s for clips over 2.5 min), with the timecode burned in. Large versions are named `<ID>_large.jpg`.

Status codes: **L** = link only, **D** = downloaded, **V** = downloaded and viewed on a contact sheet.

## 1. Recommendation (short)

**Main story: PO3 Brendan Kiley, Coast Guard rescue swimmer, Air Station Cape Cod.** He tells the whole film's arc in his own voice in a public-domain Coast Guard interview (DVIDS 620835):
- he arrives as a young swimmer with no big search-and-rescue case behind him;
- on a one-story roof a mother pushes a plastic tub at him: "that's my babies in there";
- he sees helicopters "everywhere" across the city.

Independent sources confirm the facts:
- an AFRAS award citation;
- ABC13 (Sept 4, 2017);
- the Notre Dame alumni magazine.

Footage from the same days (Aug 27–29), the same aircraft type (MH-60 Jayhawk) and the same unit is public domain:
- Cape Cod Jayhawk window photos 3709576 and 3709578, taken Aug 27;
- the Coast Guard hoist footage listed in section 2A.

Two other Cape Cod voices from the same 2018 interview series can support him:
- Lt. Cmdr. Simon Greene (620838): pilots navigating by Google Maps to 911 addresses, landing in ballparks when rain bands hit;
- Lt. Michelle Cosenza (620854): her first real hoist, a family of four with a calm baby.

These three let us go back to one crew as the flood grows.

**Secondary 1: CPO Matthew Dusek, US Coast Guard, Dickinson.** His own house flooded. He took his boat and spent the day pulling neighbours out. Days later he is on camera in his gutted street (551726): "I don't have a house. I'm homeless." This is the civilian/homeowner side, told by someone who appears in PD footage.

**Secondary 2, choose one:**
- **Lt. Dan Warren, 212th Rescue Squadron, Alaska Air National Guard, Vidor** (547400). A clean on-camera interview at the flood's edge, plus matching boat and high-water-truck b-roll (547430). This covers the Beaumont/Vidor/Orange phase (Aug 30–31).
- **The Addicks/Barker reservoir thread.** USACE aerials (547036, 547295) and Col. Zetterstrom's press conference (549709). This is a "system" story with no single named victim in PD footage.

Press-only stories (Sgt. Steve Perez, the Saldivar family) are best told in narration over neutral PD footage, with no images of the people. See section 3.

  - Aviation Survival Technician 3rd Class;
  - Notre Dame '10, from Muskegon, Michigan;
  - stationed at Air Station Cape Cod.
- **What:**
  - Aug 27–29, 2017: crew of CGNR 6026 (MH-60 Jayhawk). They flew from Lafayette, LA into Houston through the outer bands.
  - 112 people rescued in 21.3 flight hours; 18 deployments by Kiley from over 100 ft.
  - The tub rescue: on day 2, a woman on a one-story roof pushed a plastic storage bin at him. Inside were her 2-year-old holding a newborn. He carried the bin down, then helped the mother. All three went into the basket.
