# TikTok covers — house rules (used for all episodes)

- Size 1080x1920 (9:16), PNG/JPG. Upload it in the app: post screen -> Edit cover -> upload image (or pick a frame).
- Profile grid shows only a centred ~3:4 crop (about y 240..1680): title and the main object must sit inside it.
- In the feed the cover sits under the UI: keep the title and hero out of the caption area (y > ~1500) and the right-hand buttons (x > ~940, y ~800..1500).
- One headline of 3-5 words in 2-3 lines, high contrast, one key word on a red strip; one hero object; max 3-4 elements.
- The cover must match the video (no misleading clickbait).
- Brands: naming a brand in commentary is fine, but don't put a brand's logo/mark on the cover in a way that could look official or affiliated
  (the McDonald's cover uses a "$" sign and "BURGERS" instead of the arches).
