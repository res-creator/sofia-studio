DESCRIPTION
On January 15, 1919, a steel tank holding 2.3 million gallons of molasses burst in Boston's North End. The wave tore through the streets at around 35 mph, killing 21 people and injuring about 150. This is the story of the warning signs nobody listened to, the rescue, the trial, and what the real science says about why it happened.
