YOUTUBE SHORTS
Title: He Paid 10,000 Bitcoins for 2 Pizzas (Worth $1.26 Billion at the Peak)
Description: On May 18, 2010, programmer Laszlo Hanyecz in Jacksonville, Florida offered 10,000 bitcoins on the BitcoinTalk forum for a couple of pizzas. Four days later a teenager in California had two large Papa John's delivered to him. The coins were worth about $41 then; the pizzas cost the buyer about $25. At bitcoin's peak on Oct 6, 2025, the same 10,000 BTC were worth about $1.26 billion (about $747 million on Nov 9, 2026). Laszlo — who says he wrote the first GPU miner — made the same offer again, then wrote that he couldn't afford to keep doing it. In all, he says he spent about 100,000 bitcoins, much of it on pizza. His answer to the "what if": "You know, I don't regret it." (Illustrated re-enactment; generic characters, no likenesses. Brand names as text only.)
