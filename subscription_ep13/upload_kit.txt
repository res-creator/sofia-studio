YOUTUBE SHORTS
Title: The Comcast call that wouldn't end: 18 minutes to cancel internet (2014)
Description: In July 2014, Veronica Belmont and her husband Ryan Block called Comcast to disconnect their internet. The retention employee kept asking why. About ten minutes in, Ryan took the phone and recorded the rest — roughly 18 minutes in all. The recording went viral, Comcast apologized and said the way the employee spoke was "not consistent with how we train," and Ryan asked the company not to fire him. A week later, an internal memo from Comcast's COO leaked: the employee "did a lot of what we trained him and paid him... to do." The memo also said saving a customer must be handled with respect. Animated re-enactment; quotes from the recording and the documents.
