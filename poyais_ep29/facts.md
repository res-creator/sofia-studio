# Ep 29 — The Country That Didn't Exist (Gregor MacGregor, Poyais, 1822–23) — verified facts

| Fact | Source (opened) |
|---|---|
| 29 Apr 1820: the Mosquito king George Frederic Augustus grants MacGregor ~8,000,000 acres for "rum and jewellery"; grant later revoked | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregor_MacGregor |
| MacGregor styles himself "Cazique" (ruler) of Poyais; Poyais as a state with capital St Joseph was his invention | Wikipedia (above) |
| 1822: 355-page guidebook "Sketch of the Mosquito Shore, Including the Territory of Poyais" by a "Captain Thomas Strangeways" — probably written by MacGregor or accomplices | Wikipedia |
| Guidebook: Poyais "one of the most healthy and beautiful spots in the world" | https://www.history.com/articles/the-con-man-who-invented-his-own-country |
| Bank of Poyais dollar notes printed by the Bank of Scotland's official printer | Wikipedia; https://www.mentalfloss.com/article/536237/how-scottish-swindler-lured-his-countrymen-fake-city-untold-riches |
| 23 Oct 1822: £200,000 Poyais loan offered in London (Sir John Perring, Shaw, Barber & Co.), bonds at 80%, 6% interest; most buyers missed later instalments | Wikipedia |
| Land: 2s 3d per acre, just over £11 for 100 acres | Mental Floss; History.com (100 acres for £11) |
| Titles/jobs sold; a cobbler made "Shoemaker to the Princess" of Poyais; commissions in Poyais army/navy bought | Mental Floss |
| Settlers "happily traded their gold and legal tender" for Poyais dollars | Mental Floss; Wikipedia ("exchange their gold for the legal currency of Poyais") |
| Ships: Honduras Packet (London, 10 Sep 1822, ~70) and Kennersley Castle (Leith, Jan 1823, ~200) | Wikipedia; Mental Floss |
| Found: no capital, only old foundations/rubble and jungle | Wikipedia; History.com |
| ~250 emigrated; "at least 180 had perished"; fewer than 50 returned to Britain (accounts differ: "more than half", "two-thirds") | Wikipedia; History.com |
| The cobbler shot himself | Mental Floss |
| 1826: French court tries him for fraud — acquitted | Wikipedia; History.com |
| 1827: new £800,000 Poyais loan; handbill "Another Poyais Humbug" | Wikipedia |
| Moved to Venezuela 1838, military pension, died Caracas 4 Dec 1845, buried with full military honours; never convicted | Wikipedia; History.com |
| Economist (2012): "the greatest confidence trick of all time" | Wikipedia (cites) |
