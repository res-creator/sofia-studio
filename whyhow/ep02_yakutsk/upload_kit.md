## Description
```
Step outside in Yakutsk in January and it's −50°C. Your breath turns to ice, boiling water turns to snow, and bare skin can freeze in minutes. Yet 355,000 people live here, go to work, and keep their apartments at +20°C.

How? We counted seven tricks — from a city that works as one giant freezer, to cars that never get switched off, a river with no bridge, pipes lifted off the ground, and the most important one, hidden under every building: keeping the ground itself frozen.
```
