## Description
```
Norilsk is a city of 174,000 people, 300 km north of the Arctic Circle, with no road to the rest of Russia — and every building stands on frozen ground. So why don't the houses sink? And what happens when the ice starts losing?

We cut the ground open: permafrost, piles frozen into the ice, the ventilated gap under every block, the cracked buildings, the 2020 diesel spill, and the steel pipes that refrigerate the earth under the city.
```
