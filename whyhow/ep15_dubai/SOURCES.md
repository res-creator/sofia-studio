# EP15 How Dubai keeps a city alive in the desert: sources

Researched 2026-11-25. Confidence: H = a primary source states it, or several solid sources agree; M = figures vary by source/date or the source is secondary; L = estimate / single secondary source / unsourced popular figure, say it on screen as "about" / "reportedly".
Wikipedia was used only as a pointer; no number below rests on Wikipedia alone.
**Access notes**: dewa.gov.ae (all PDFs and many news pages) returns HTTP 403 to automated fetch, and wam.ae pages render empty. DEWA figures below were read through the Dubai Media Office (mediaoffice.ae) copies of the same DEWA releases, through DFM filings, or through search snapshots of dewa.gov.ae pages; marked where relevant.
**Units warning**: DEWA uses **imperial** gallons (MIGD = million imperial gallons per day; 1 imp gal = 4.546 L = 1.20 US gal). For a US audience, convert before putting a gallon figure on screen (see Scale comparisons).
**Big flag for the cold open**: the famous **254.8 mm** fell at **Khatm Al Shakla, Al Ain (Abu Dhabi emirate)**, not in Dubai. Dubai's own gauges read **~142-168 mm** (Dubai airport ~164 mm). Say "the UAE" for 254.8 mm, "Dubai" for ~160 mm.

## Popular claims checked

| Claim | Verdict | Notes / source # |
|---|---|---|
| "Dubai got 254 mm of rain on 16 April 2024" | **Wrong place** | 254.8 mm was at Khatm Al Shakla, **Al Ain** (Abu Dhabi emirate). Dubai: ~142-168 mm. (#2, #3) |
| "A year's rain in a day" | **True for Dubai, even an understatement** | ~164 mm vs 94.7 mm average = ~1.7 years. (#3) |
| "Cloud seeding caused the April 2024 flood" | **False** | NCM: no seeding during the storm; WWA: seeding can't add that much moisture. (#8) |
| "Dubai makes rain with cloud seeding" | **Half true** | The **UAE's NCM** seeds existing clouds, ~10-15 % more rain from a suitable cloud; it cannot create rain, and it's not a Dubai city programme. (#43) |
| "100 % of Dubai's water is desalinated" | **Almost** | Drinking water is overwhelmingly desalinated (495 MIGD capacity); DEWA also keeps ~36 MIGD of groundwater wells as a strategic reserve. Say "almost all". (#14) |
| "Jebel Ali is the world's largest desalination plant" | **True (single site)** | Guinness 2022: largest single-site desalination facility, 490 MIGD. Hassyan will be the largest **RO** plant. (#15, #17) |
| "Air conditioning uses 70 % of Dubai's electricity" | **Unsourced** | DEWA says AC is "as much as 60 %" of **peak** load. (#22) |
| "Dubai got rich on oil" | **Only at the start** | Oil (1966/1969) paid for the creek, port and power plants; output peaked 1991; now ~1 % of GDP. Abu Dhabi holds the UAE's oil. (#31, #32) |
| "Oil is under 1 % of Dubai's GDP" | **Roughly true** | Secondary sources say <1 %; OBG said 1.3 % (2016). Say "about one percent". (#32) |
| "My grandfather rode a camel... his son will ride a camel" (Sheikh Rashid) | **Unproven attribution** | AAP FactCheck: attributed to Saudi minister Yamani as early as 1986; Sheikh Hamdan told a similar story; the "hard times" add-on is from a 2016 novel. Don't quote it as his words. (#49) |
| "Dubai had 20,000 people in 1950" | **Unverified** | Only secondary; first census 1968 = 59,000. (#40) |
| "Dubai has 3.8-4 million people" | **Out of date** | Official end-2025: **4.58 m**. (#41) |
| "Palm Jumeirah was built from desert sand" | **False** | Seabed sand from the Gulf; desert sand is too round. (#37, #38) |
| "Dubai imports sand" | **True** | For concrete, yes; desert grains are wind-polished. (#38) |
| "Jebel Ali port is visible from space" | **Press cliché** | Repeated by Gulf News, not a measured claim; skip. (#34) |
| "Dubai imports 90 % of its food" | **True for the UAE** | 85-90 % (UAE-wide). (#26) |
| "The solar park is 5 GW" | **Not yet** | 3.86 GW now; 5 GW is a mid-term goal in older releases, 7.26-8+ GW by 2030. (#23) |

## Cold open options

1. **Best: "Tuesday, 16 April 2024"** - A city that makes almost all its water from the sea, built for no rain. Open on the airport: planes taxiing like boats; 1,244 flights cancelled. Then the twist: in that flood, Ann Celestial's building in Al Barsha had **no tap water**, and residents scooped water from the pool. Line: "Dubai gets less rain in a year than Phoenix. That Tuesday it got almost two years of it in a day, and the one thing the city could not do was get rid of water." Then: "To understand why, you have to understand how this city stays alive every other day of the year." (#1-6, #46)
2. **Alt: "1 August 2025, 3 pm, 125 °F"** - the August record at Sweihan; that same summer DEWA's peak hit record levels, up to 60 % of it air conditioning. Strong but less visual than the flood.
3. **Alt: "June 1979"** - the first glass of desalinated water and the Queen's port opening at the same sand spit, Jebel Ali. Better as the Act 2 hinge than as an opener.
