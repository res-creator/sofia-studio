## Honesty notes
- The 1965 press conference was a recorded broadcast, not live TV. The recording was stopped.
- Lee Kuan Yew wept over the end of the merger, not over water. The film says he "raised" the water problem minutes earlier.
- NEWater mostly goes to industry. The film says "clean enough to drink".
- The Tekong polder is for army training, not homes.
