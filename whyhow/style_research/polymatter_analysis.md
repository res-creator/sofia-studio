# PolyMatter editing style (vidIQ watch, 3 videos, Nov 2026)
Videos: KXvVyd2wmdQ (Singapore elections), uaKN006y0tI (Hong Kong economy), QEVTeJtzJFo (new car $50,000).
- 60–80% real footage (drone, street, archive, article screenshots), 20–40% flat motion graphics.
- Palette: deep crimson/red or slate blue backgrounds with subtle film grain / paper texture; white text, yellow/amber highlight on key numbers.
- Type: bold geometric sans (Montserrat/Proxima-like), all caps headings; text 1.5–5 s; cut in or slide/scale in.
- Charts: flat, white axes on dark grid; lines draw left-to-right with narration; bars grow; number counters.
- Maps: flat or 3D relief, white outlines, dashed borders, white arrows.
- Shots 1.5–4 s (6–8 s for drone/maps); mostly hard cuts, few dissolves/whips; cuts follow speech, accents on music hits.
- Stills: slow push-ins/pans. Article screenshots with highlighted phrases.
- Music: ambient electronic with light driving percussion.
- No full chapter cards: transitions via full-screen map/diagram.
- Motifs: white bracket boxes/arrows on footage, checkmarks, split screens, white vector cut-outs, counters.
