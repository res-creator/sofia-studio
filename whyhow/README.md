## Current production rules (from EP11, Nov 2026)
- Voice: Kokoro am_puck, **speed 1.0, pause 0.4** (~145 wpm; the owner approved slowing down from 1.08), 0.45 s between paragraphs. Target 11–15 min.
- Script: the 5-move hook + roadmap, micro-hooks every 3–5 min, one named person per chapter, bookend ending, personal CTA (see `07_reference_breakdown_nov2026.md`).
- Graphics: relief globe (`globe` with `relief`, `routes`, `waves`, `zones`, `labels`), `supplyline` diagram, full-screen quote cards. Hold quote cards ~6 s and keep diagrams moving.
- Sound: `mix.json` `drop` (silence before a shock line), per-shot `amb` (sea/storm/rain/wind) and `sfx` (click on archival photos, boom, whoosh, riser).
- Check every archival clip for titles, leaders and hidden extra shots before using it (frames every 1 s).
