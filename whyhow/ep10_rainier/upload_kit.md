## Description
```
Every couple of years, thousands of kids in Washington's Puyallup Valley leave class and walk uphill, some of them two miles. They're practicing for the day Mount Rainier sends a river of mud down the valley. In Orting, after the alarm, they'd have about 40 minutes. And the mud doesn't need an eruption.

We look at the rotten rock inside Rainier, the Osceola Mudflow that pushed back Puget Sound, the forest buried standing up under Orting (tree rings say it died in late summer 1507), what Armero in Colombia taught the world in 1985, the lahar sirens and the new USGS timing map, the biggest lahar drill on Earth, and the one scenario nobody can answer yet.
```
