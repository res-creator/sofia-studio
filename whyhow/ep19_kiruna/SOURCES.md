Honesty notes
- "Slower than you walk": the church averaged ~0.5 km/h (walking ≈ 5 km/h).
- "About a hundred elephants": 672 t ÷ ~6 t per adult African elephant ≈ 110.
- "Deeper than two Empire State Buildings": level 1365 is roughly 1,100 m below the surface; 2 × 443 m = 886 m.
- "More than twenty historic buildings have made the trip": AP counts 25 moved before the church; others count 23 incl. the church.
- Distances: new centre ≈ 3 km (2 mi) east; church route ≈ 5 km (3 mi).
