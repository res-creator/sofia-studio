## Honesty notes
- "No fresh water": Key West has a thin freshwater lens and old wells, but no usable supply for a city; the film says "almost no fresh water underground".
- 130 miles / 45 bridge crossings are FKAA's pipeline figures; the highway itself is 113 miles with 42 bridges.
- The 1942 pipe was 18 inches and 128 miles; the larger 36/30/24-inch line came in 1982.
- The 1860s still: 7,000 gallons a day ≈ 20 homes, using EPA's ~300 gallons per family per day.
- Pipe replacement cost "~$10 million a mile" is the FKAA director's estimate as reported; the first 4 miles cost ~$42 million.
- Generic shots are labelled ILLUSTRATIVE; the pipeline photos are real (1960, Hurricane Donna).
