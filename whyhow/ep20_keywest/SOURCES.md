Honesty notes
- The pipe count "forty-five bridge crossings" (FKAA) differs from the highway's "42 bridges"; the film uses FKAA's number for the pipe.
- 1942 pipe: sources differ between "18-inch" (Navy line, keyshistory) and the 36/30/24-inch line of 1982 (both are in the film, with dates).
- The water barge period is described only generally ("by boat, in big tanks"), as sources give no detail.
- Generic shots (taps, showers, pools, storms, reefs, a sea bridge) are labelled ILLUSTRATIVE.
