## Honesty notes
- "40 million" is trips, not people: transfers between companies count twice.
- We do not claim "zero Shinkansen deaths". We say that only one derailment has ever happened with passengers on board (2004), and nobody was hurt.
- Tessei (the seven-minute cleaning) works on JR East trains. The footage in that part shows JR East E7/E3 trains, not the Tokaido line.
- Tokyu profit figures come from a secondary aggregator. They are labelled "reported".
