## Description
```
Venice stands in a salt-water lagoon with no solid ground under it — only sand and soft mud. Yet for more than a thousand years it has held up stone palaces, churches and bell towers. The secret: a forest under the streets. Millions of tree trunks, driven into the mud by hand, still holding up the city.

So why hasn't the wood rotted? Why doesn't Venice sink? And why is it sinking anyway? We go from the upside-down forest to boat ambulances, acqua alta sirens, the 78 steel gates of MOSE and the flood of tourists.
```
