## Honesty notes
- "Phoenix" water split (58/40/2) is the City of Phoenix's own; other valley cities differ.
- Hohokam canal mileage is an estimate (500–1,000 miles depending on what is counted); the film says "hundreds of miles … maybe close to a thousand".
- Phoenix itself is about 190 canal miles from Lake Havasu; 336 miles is the whole CAP to Tucson. The ~3,000 ft lift is the total to the Tucson end.
- Palo Verde is "one of" the biggest US nuclear plants (Vogtle in Georgia is now larger by capacity).
- The 4.86 million acre-feet figure is the June 2023 model; a 2024 update lowered it to ~3.6 million.
- Solar-over-canal stock shots and cooling towers are ILLUSTRATIVE, not the Gila River project or Palo Verde (Palo Verde has its own real aerial photo).
