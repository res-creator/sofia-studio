## Description
```
When the Angel of Independence was finished in 1910, you climbed nine steps to reach its base. Today there are fourteen more. Nobody made the monument taller — the city around it sank.

Mexico City sits on the bottom of a drained lake. It drinks the water under its own streets, the lake clay squeezes like a sponge, and in places the ground drops by up to half a metre a year — more than a hundred times faster than Venice. We look at the Aztec city on the lake, the flood of 1629, the leaning cathedral, the palace that sank metres into the clay, the 1985 earthquake, and why a city built on a lake is running out of water.
```
