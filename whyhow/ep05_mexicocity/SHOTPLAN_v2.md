Built 17 Nov 2026 for `script_v2.md` (X001–X035). Keys = `footage/manifest.json`.
New items for v2 have `"added": "v2"` and an `is_real_subject` field; contact sheets: `footage/_sheet_v2_1.jpg` … `_sheet_v2_N.jpg`.

**Legend:** **R** = real photo/video of the actual subject named in the line ·
**S** = real Mexico City material, but a stand-in (not the exact thing the voice names) — needs an honest label ·
**A** = real historical document (old map, painting, engraving) ·
**D** = our own diagram/animation (no AI video, no AI images).
`(v)` = video clip (re-encoded h264 ≤1920 px, no audio). Everything else is a still (use slow push/pan).

Lower-third rule: any archive/stand-in shot whose place or year differs from what the voice says gets a small on-screen note (text given below).
