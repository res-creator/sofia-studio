# Step 6 — How to stay clear of YouTube's "inauthentic content" rule

## What the rule actually says (YouTube Help, monetization policies, checked 12 Nov 2026)
On 15 July 2025 YouTube renamed "repetitious content" to **"inauthentic content"**. It is a YPP (monetization) rule — a channel that breaks it
is not removed, but gets **no money** (rejected at YPP review or demonetized later).
Not allowed, quoted from the policy page:
- "Similar or repetitive content with low educational value, commentary, narratives, or minimal variation across videos"
- "Image slideshows, templated storylines, or scrolling text with minimal or no narrative"
- "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production"
- Reused content: repurposing what's already on YouTube without "a meaningful difference".
Allowed: the same intro/outro if "the bulk of your content is different"; "each video has a distinct storyline, focus, or concept";
using AI to help with scripts. **AI voice itself is not banned.**
Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 · AI disclosure: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491

## Separate rule: "altered or synthetic content" label
Must tick "Yes" in YouTube Studio when a video contains **realistic** AI or altered content a viewer could take for real
(a real person saying something they didn't, realistic fake footage of a real place or event).
Not needed for: AI text-to-speech narration over real footage, obvious illustrations/animation, colour correction, our diagrams.
The label does not reduce reach or monetization (YouTube Help). Our plan: no realistic AI images → normally "No"; if we ever use one → "Yes".

## Our 12 rules (do all of them, every video)
1. **One real question per video, a real answer.** Each film has its own story arc, not a filled-in template.
2. **Unique script** written for the topic; no stock sentences reused across videos (only the 5-second channel tag at the end).
3. **Sources in every description** (papers, government data, journalism) — shows "educational value".
4. **Our own graphics ≥ 40–50 % of screen time**: maps, cross-sections, data charts we build. Not a slideshow of photos.
5. **No reused YouTube/TV footage.** Only free-licence or our own material, credited.
6. **Commentary and analysis**: explain *why*, compare, name and check myths, show where sources disagree.
7. **Human editorial control**: the owner approves every script and watches every final cut before upload; keep `script.md` history in git.
8. **Quality over quantity**: 1–2 films a week, not daily filler; never upload near-identical videos ("Top 10 coldest… part 7").
9. **Varied formats**: Why / How / What if / myth-check, different structures and lengths (12–20 min) depending on the topic.
10. **Honest metadata**: titles and thumbnails only promise what the video shows; no fake numbers on thumbnails.
11. **Transparency line** in every description: "Narration is created with AI text-to-speech. Research, script, maps and graphics are made by our team.
    Facts are checked against the sources below."
12. **Community**: pin a real question, answer comments, fix errors with a pinned correction (shows a real channel behind the videos).

## Before applying for YPP (1,000 subs AND either 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months or 10M Shorts views in 90 days)
- At least 8–10 documentaries in the new format, all following the rules above.
- Old money-story Shorts are fine (they are original); old long videos too.
- Re-read the policy page right before applying — YouTube changes wording often.
