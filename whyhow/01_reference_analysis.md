## Title formulas that work
- `Why is [remote place] MORE DANGEROUS / HARDER TO REACH than [famous place]? N reasons` — comparison with a world-famous rival + a countdown.
- `Why don't [houses/yurts] freeze at −50°C?` — everyday life + an extreme number.
- `How long could you survive [alone] in/on X?` · `What if you were born in X?` · `Why is it almost IMPOSSIBLE to…`
- `…[the obvious cause] isn't the main thing` (open loop).
- 4–10 words, Title Case, 1–2 words in CAPS (in English: use CAPS on one word at most, or none — English YouTube reads ALL CAPS as clickbait).

## Structure (from transcripts)
- **No greeting, no channel intro.** First 30 s = 2–3 surprising numbers + the question / promise ("12 reasons, and the last one…").
- Body: either a **countdown (12 → 1)** with a concrete figure + comparison in each reason (Неприступная Земля), a **chronological walk** (¿Y si?),
  or a **continuous explanation** with a cinematic cold open ("Polar night. −50°. Inside, people walk barefoot. How?") (Хроники аномалий).
- Facts every few sentences; comparisons with famous places.
- CTA only in the last 10 s: "Which place should we cover next? Write in the comments."

## What we take from them
1. Arctic / cold / "how do people live there" topics are the strongest proven niche → our first films: Norilsk, taiga, Oymyakon, Longyearbyen.
2. Hook = extreme number + a question in the first 30 s; open loop paid off at the end.
3. Comparison with a famous place and numbered countdowns — use in some films (variety!), not all.
4. Length 12–20 min (English, between ¿Y si? and the Russian 25-min norm).
5. CTA only at the end, as "which place next?".

## What we do differently (and why)
- **No photoreal AI thumbnails / visuals** — two of the three channels rely on them; it is exactly the "mass-produced AI" pattern YouTube is cracking down on,
  and realistic fake images of real places would need the synthetic-content label. Our "Cutaway" thumbnails (real photo/map + our cross-section) keep the
  warm-vs-cold contrast and the cutaway idea that worked for the yurt video, honestly.
- **Not daily.** Хроники аномалий shows the decay: copies of one formula went 4.8M → 577K → 100K → under 1K. 1–2 strong films a week instead.
- **Sources in every description** — none of the three shows them; it is our trust and anti-"inauthentic" signal.
